# Elifba frontend — UI rules

How Elifba screens look and behave. The rules are adapted from the owner's precedent (the owner's earlier Laravel project) to an app with three kinds of screen: the teacher's pages (laptop and phone), the **board** on the classroom wall, and the **remote** on the teacher's phone. Change this file first when a rule changes; a review checks new UI against it.

Step A2 shipped the starter kit's screens (welcome, authentication, settings), all translated. The kit's empty dashboard is gone: a signed-in teacher lands on Siniflər. The rules below apply to every screen added from A3 on.

## Building blocks

| Layer      | Where                                                                                    | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tokens     | `resources/css/app.css`                                                                  | Colours, radius, sidebar and chart tokens for light and `.dark`; `font-sans` (Inter) for the interface and `font-board` (Andika) for text children read, both self-hosted in `resources/fonts/`.                                                                                                                                               |
| Primitives | `resources/js/components/ui`                                                             | shadcn/ui from the starter kit. Not linted or formatted; keep them close to upstream. Their few built-in words (`Close`, `More`…) use `useTranslation()`.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Shared     | `resources/js/components`                                                                | The shell (`app-header`, `app-sidebar`, `bottom-nav`, `area-pills`, `user-menu`, `breadcrumbs`; the nav definition in `components/app/nav-areas.ts`, the roles' icons in `components/app/roles.ts`), the kit's parts (`heading`, `text-link`, `input-error`…) and Elifba's own (`language-switcher`). From A3 a kit grows in `components/app`. |
| Features   | `resources/js/components/{classes,students,children,games,rankings,share,welcome,rooms}` | Screens built from the shared parts (A3 on; `children` from R4; `rooms`, the player area's own, from B3 and kept apart, rule 15).                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Game       | `resources/js/game/`                                                                     | The board's engine, contract and Phaser scenes (A6 on); React owns everything around the canvas.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Use a shared component when one fits; add to the shared layer instead of copying markup into a feature folder.

## Rules

### 1. Layouts

- `resources/js/app.tsx` picks the layout by page name, through the pure `pageShell()` of `lib/page-shell.ts` (F2; `page-shell.test.ts` holds it, and every `rooms/*` page must be named there as in the shell or not): `welcome`, `privacy` and `credits` (public pages) have none, `auth/*` uses `AuthLayout`, `settings/*` uses `AppLayout` + `SettingsLayout`, everything else `AppLayout` (sidebar shell). The board (`games/board` from A6, `lovhe` from A7), the remote (`games/remote`, A7), the phone's pairing pages (`pult/*`, A7), a room's screen (`rooms/screen`, B4, rule 15) and, from F2, a room's page (`rooms/show`, ADR 032, rule 15 "The game frame") have no app shell: full screen, no sidebar. The rest of the player area — `rooms/index`, `rooms/past`, `rooms/join` — keeps `AppLayout`; from I2 the invitation's door, `rooms/invited` (ADR 033), is drawn in the auth card (`AuthLayout`) and named in `page-shell.ts` as a room page in it.
- There is no dashboard. A signed-in account lands through `/start` (Fortify's `home`, architecture §1 "Accounts and roles"): a teacher on Siniflər (`/classes`), a parent on Uşaqlarım (`/children`, rule 12), a player on Oyunlar (`/rooms`, B2, G3, architecture §12), an account without a role on the role choice (`/role`, `auth/choose-role`: two cards, «Müəllim» and «Valideyn», and «Oyunçu» while the rooms area is on, then that role's agreement). `/dashboard` is an unnamed 302 to `/start` for old bookmarks.
- `AppLayout` is the teacher shell, after the owner's precedent. It navigates between **areas** and the **pages** inside an area, never with browser-style work tabs or a "More" sheet:
    - **One nav definition**, `components/app/nav-areas.ts`, one navigation per area (R2): the teacher's Siniflər (`/classes`), Sözlər (`/words`) and Ayarlar; the parent's Uşaqlarım (`/children`) and the same Ayarlar (Profil, Təhlükəsizlik, Görünüş və dil, Oyun — `/settings/play`, the phone remote on or off, architecture §4 "Pairing" — and Rollar, `/settings/roles`); the player's Oyunlar (`/rooms`, B2) and Keçmiş oyunlar (`/rooms/past`, G3) and Ayarlar without Oyun, which is the boards'; each with a translation key, a lucide icon and its pages. The sidebar, the bottom bar and the pill strip all read the navigation of the page's area (`useNavAreas()`, from the shared `auth.area`: a page inside an area shows that area's, the shared and account pages the account's); an account without a role has none. The open area and page come from the URL (`lib/nav-active.ts`, covered by Vitest), never from stored state: every page under `/classes` (a class, its rankings, podium, game setup) belongs to Siniflər, and every page under `/children` (a child, a home game's setup) to Uşaqlarım. A new teacher page joins an area by its URL; a new area is one entry in the definition.
    - **Header** (sticky, `h-14`, every viewport): the sidebar toggle (`lg+` only), the brand linking home (`/start`), and on the right the user menu (`UserMenu`): name and email, the area switch (Rol: Müəllim / Valideyn / Oyunçu, `PUT /area`; only for an account with more than one role), language, theme, Ayarlar and Çıxış.
    - **`lg+` (≥1024px)**: the collapsible icon sidebar (`collapsible="icon"`, open or closed in the `sidebar_state` cookie, `SidebarRail` on its edge). An area with one page is a link; an area with more is a collapsible group, open while one of its pages is, and collapsed to an icon it lists its pages in a flyout menu. The sidebar holds navigation only.
    - **Below `lg`**: no sidebar (`useIsMobile` breaks at 1024 and the toggle is hidden, so the drawer cannot open). A fixed bottom tab bar (`BottomNav`) switches areas and marks the open one; it pads for the safe area, and the content is padded so nothing hides under it. An area with two or more pages shows them as a sticky pill strip under the header (`AreaPills`; today only Ayarlar). `SettingsLayout` has no nav list of its own.
    - **Breadcrumbs** show under the header on every viewport when a page has two or more, so a deep class page always has a way back (Siniflər › 3A › Reytinq). A single crumb repeats the page title and is not shown.
- **Auth pages.** Every `auth/*` page shares `layouts/auth/auth-simple-layout.tsx`. On `lg+` a quiet brand side (5/12 of the width, `green-50`, dark `green-950/30`) carries the logo linking home, a few of the logo's balloons as faint decoration and the welcome page's promise (`welcome.hero.title`, `welcome.hero.who`); the form column has the language switch at the top right, the page's title and description centred over a `max-w-sm` column, and the footer (Məxfilik, Müəlliflər). On a phone it is one column: the logo and the compact language switch, the page, the footer.
    - Login and registration put Google first when the instance offers it (the `googleSignIn` prop, ADR 008): the full-width «Google ilə daxil ol» / «Google ilə qeydiyyatdan keç» button (`GoogleSignIn`), a quiet «və ya e-poçt ilə» divider (`AuthDivider`), then the e-mail form, and the link to the other page last. Without Google the form stands alone, with no divider, and the first field takes the focus. A server `status` shows above everything in `AuthStatus`.
    - The Google button follows Google's sign-in branding guidelines and nothing comes from Google: the light theme (white, a 1px `#747775` border, `#1F1F1F` text) and in dark mode the dark one (`#131314`, `#8E918F`, `#E3E3E3`) with the unchanged «G» on its white circle, 12px padding, Google's approved wording translated (`auth.google.sign_in`, `sign_up`, `confirm`). Its text is Inter, not Google Sans: no font loads from Google, and Google Sans is not in `resources/fonts/`.
    - Tab order is the DOM order; no explicit `tabIndex`. «Şifrəni unutmusunuz?» sits on the «Məni xatırla» row, after the password.
    - «Məni xatırla» (`RememberMe`) is ticked when the page opens, with «Ortaq kompüterdə bu işarəni götürün.» under it, and the page keeps its state: the same box posts `remember` with the e-mail form and sets the Google link's `?remember=1|0` (architecture §7 "Remembered login", ADR 017). Registration has it after the password confirmation, before the privacy-note tick.
    - Settings → Təhlükəsizlik has «Başqa cihazlar» under the password form: one line of what it does and an `outline` «Başqa cihazlardan çıx» button, no dialog (nothing is deleted; the page already asked for the password), and a toast when done.
- A page sets its layout props statically: `Login.layout = { title: 'auth.login.title', description: 'auth.login.description' }` or `Profile.layout = { breadcrumbs: [{ title: 'settings.profile.title', href: edit() }] }`. Those values are **translation keys**; the layout translates them. A page that changes them at run time calls `setLayoutProps` with keys too.
- Nav items (`NavItem.title`) and breadcrumbs (`BreadcrumbItem.title`) are translation keys as well, translated where they are rendered.

### 2. Language

- Every interface string is in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json` (Azerbaijani is the base) and is read with `const { t } = useTranslation()`. The parity test fails on a missing key, an extra key, an empty value or different `:placeholders`.
- Keys are dotted and grouped by screen: `auth.login.*`, `settings.profile.*`, `nav.*`, `common.*` for words used everywhere (`common.save`, `common.cancel`, `common.password`…). Reuse a `common.*` key before adding a new one.
- Placeholders use `:name`; `t('words.edit.button', { word })`. Plurals use `{one}…|{other}…` with a numeric `count`; Azerbaijani keeps the noun singular after a number, so its two forms are usually equal.
- Never join translated fragments into a sentence: Azerbaijani word order differs from English. Give the whole sentence one key with placeholders.
- The language switch sits in the auth pages' footer, the welcome page's header and Settings → Appearance and language (`LanguageSwitcher`), and in the teacher shell's user menu in the header (`UserMenu`), on every viewport. Both call `PUT /locale`; the server keeps the choice.
- Child-facing game text (prompts, words, sentences) is **content**, always Azerbaijani, and never goes into `lang/*.json`.
- Write in plain words a teacher uses. Code comments are English.

### 3. Page header and actions

- Each page starts with one `h1` or a `Heading` and, beside it, the page's main action. A short intro is one line under the title.
- Each teacher page declares its breadcrumbs through its layout props.

### 4. Buttons

- The main action of a form or page is the default `<Button>`; secondary controls are `outline` or `ghost`; deleting is `destructive` and asks first in a `Dialog`.
- A navigating action is an Inertia `<Link>` (with `Button asChild` when it should look like a button), never a button with `router.visit`. The one exception leaves the app: the Google buttons (`GoogleSignIn`) are plain `<a>` links to our own `/auth/google/*` routes, whose answer is a redirect to Google (architecture §7 "Google sign-in").
- A form's submit button shows `Spinner` while processing and is disabled meanwhile.
- On the board and the remote, targets are big (at least 64px on the remote's item buttons) and never depend on hover.
- A prompt the board says (architecture §3 "Voice") has a round 🔊 button (`Volume2`) after its text, framed in the pack's accent like the target, 64px on the projector (80px from `xl`, 44px below `lg`), labelled «Tapşırığı yenidən səsləndir»; it says the prompt again. There is none when the round has no clip, the account turned the prompt's voice off or the board is silent. The voice itself belongs to the canvas (`voice.ts`, `BoardScene`), never to React.
- The board's silent mode (architecture §4 "Board in the browser") is a toggle button labelled «Səssiz» with `aria-pressed`: outline while the board plays sound, filled while it is silent. Its icon is a crossed-out speaker (`VolumeOff`) in both states, never `Volume2`, so it cannot be taken for the 🔊 that says the prompt again. It sits in the header and, before «Başla», on the start screen beside «Pultu qoş».
- «Kim oynayır?» (architecture §4 "Board without a phone") is a dialog with the shared `StudentGrid`, opened by «Başla» or «Davam et» in a class game with students: a tap on a choice starts the game, so it never has a confirm button. It and the footer's «Kim lövhədədir?» are one `StudentPickerDialog` (title and description as props). On a teacher board of a class game its top-right corner holds a green «Şagird əlavə et» (`UserPlus`) beside the dialog's own ✕, in one row; it opens the class page's paste flow (`PasteNames`, `components/students/`, the same code as `PasteNamesDialog`, with «Ləğv et» beside «Adları yoxla») in the same dialog, never a dialog over it. Once the names are added the flow closes and the students join the grid unchosen. Never on a guest board or in a home game.
- The board's voice (architecture §3 "Voice") belongs to the canvas, never to React: the page hands the stage who may be at the board as ids with their `name_audio` paths (`VoicePlayers`) and a fetcher with the board's own `auth` (`nameClip()`), and renders nothing for a name said. A student added on the board reaches the stage through `Stage.setPlayers()` and the controller through `setStudents()`; neither is created again, so the board is not claimed again. No name is said when «Kim oynayır?» picks a student, and nothing is voiced on the result screen's text, the podium, the rankings, the share card or the remote.
- A student's or a child's name that the board says aloud (ADR 016, `has_voice` true) carries `NameVoiceMark` after it: a 16px `Volume2` in `text-muted-foreground` (20px beside the child page's title), labelled «Lövhə bu adı səslə deyir» for screen readers and on hover. It is information, never a button, and is the same icon as 🔊 only because both mean "the board says this". A name without a clip gets no mark of its own: the class page's student list has one muted line under it when any active student's name has none (`students.voice.missing`), and the child's page one under the name (`children.voice.missing`). A name Elifba's name list spells otherwise («Lale» for «Lalə», `voice_spelling`) has `NameSpellingFix` under it on the class page's student list and on the child's page: one muted line, «Ad lüğətində belə yazılır: «Lalə»», and an `outline` «Düzəlt» button (labelled «Adı «Lalə» kimi düzəlt»; 44px on the child's page) that saves that spelling through the row's own update route, a refusal shown as a toast; never on the child card, which is a link. With `has_voice` null (the account's names or praise off) there is no mark, line or fix. Archived students and the child cards' reorder list show none.
- Settings → Oyun's voice form has three checkboxes in order, «Tapşırığı səslə de», «Tərif səsi» and «Adı səslə de», each with its hint; the third is unticked and disabled (its label dimmed) while «Tərif səsi» is off, and keeps the account's choice in its hidden field for when praise is on again.

### 5. Forms

- Forms are Inertia forms (`<Form {...action.form()}>` with Wayfinder, or `useForm`). Validation stays on the server (`*Data::rules()`, framework messages in `lang/{az,en}/validation.php`); `InputError` shows the field's message.
- Labels are visible (`Label htmlFor`); placeholders only show an example.
- Never show ids or codes as something to type, except the 6-digit pairing code (A7), which is made for that.
- A code field (`/pult`, `/oyun`, «Oyunlar») never ends in an error page: a code that opens nothing and a code tried too often are answered under the field (`pult.errors.{unknown,too_many}`, `rooms.errors.{unknown,too_many}`; architecture §4 "Codes", §12 "Rooms and joining"). The typed code stays in the field (`options={{ preserveState: 'errors' }}`), and since a link or a QR arrives by a full page load, the page reads the sentence from its own `errors` too, not only from the form's.

### 6. Spacing, grids and phones

- Every grid starts with `grid-cols-1` and adds columns from a breakpoint: `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2`.
- Nothing may widen a 375px screen: give flex children `min-w-0`, truncate long names, let wide tables scroll inside their own container.
- The teacher shell's breakpoint is `lg` (1024px): the sidebar from `lg`, the bottom tab bar and the pill strip below it. A phone and a tablet get the same navigation.
- The remote is used one-handed on a phone: its controls sit in the lower half of the screen.
- The board also opens on a phone or tablet, but it is made for the projector: from `lg` up its layout is the projector's and stays as it is; below `lg` the same parts shrink around the canvas, which keeps its 16:9 (Phaser's `Scale.FIT`; there is no portrait game layout). Below `lg` the header's controls are 44px icon buttons whose label stays for screen readers (`max-lg:sr-only`), «Keç», «Fasilə/Davam», «Səssiz» and «Bitir» among them, one tap away; the curtains and the result use smaller type; the start screen puts its title and «Başla» beside the pairing card in landscape; the pairing card stacks below `sm` with a QR of at least 144px, and during play it opens in a dialog instead of the corner of the canvas. In portrait a short hint asks to turn the phone sideways, and «Başla» tries to lock landscape after entering full screen. «Tam ekran» shows only where `document.fullscreenEnabled` is true (never on an iPhone). Leaving the board leaves full screen and unlocks the orientation, so the next page stands upright again; a guest board keeps both between its games. The «Kim oynayır?» dialog scrolls inside itself on a phone held sideways. The board page never scrolls; a curtain too tall for the screen scrolls inside itself.

### 7. Colour, type and theme

- Use the tokens (`bg-background`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-primary`, `text-destructive`…), not raw palette classes, on teacher pages. The board's look comes from the theme pack's palette (architecture §2), not from the app's tokens. A pack's backdrop (architecture §8 "Backdrops") fills the whole board page behind the header, the canvas and the footer, so its sky and ground reach the screen's edges around the 16:9 canvas. It is pale and never lowers the contrast of a tile or a letter: tiles, plates, the prompt and the footer's cards keep their own opaque faces, and text drawn straight on the page (the round count, the prompt's question) sits on its sky.
- The medal colours of places 1–3 (gold, silver, bronze; `components/share/place-colors.ts`, from OpenMoji's palette) are the one palette exception on teacher pages: the podium page and the share card use them alike, with dark text in both themes. The share card has one fixed light look and never follows dark mode.
- **The logo** (architecture §8 "Elifba's mark") is a balloon from the `find` game with «ə» cut out, drawn as SVG paths (`AppLogoIcon`, one colour from the caller's `fill-*` class). Its green, `#15803D` (`bg-green-700`), is the other palette exception: the logo's tile (white mark on green) in the header, the sidebar, the auth pages, the welcome page and the install banner (rule 11), the auth pages' brand side (`green-50`) and the welcome page's main action. The master files are `resources/images/brand/mark.svg` (one colour) and `tile.svg` (on the green tile); `public/favicon.svg` is `tile.svg`, and `public/images/og.png` shows the tile. The app icons are drawn from `tile.svg` by `php scripts/app-icons/generate.php` (architecture §10 "Installing Elifba").
- Every teacher screen works in light and dark; check both. The board is always light: its page holds `forceLightTheme()` (`use-appearance`) while it is mounted, so the dialogs it opens are light too.
- The app draws its own themes, so a browser's forced dark never repaints a page: `app.blade.php` declares `<meta name="color-scheme" content="only light">` and `html { color-scheme: only light }` (a domino tile's ivory face went dark under its black pips on Android's forced dark, ADR 038 "As built (Y8)"; `WebAppTest`). It changes nothing a browser without forced dark draws.
- Text children read — the board's prompts, items and words, the remote's item buttons — uses `font-board` (Andika, made for beginning readers: clear a, g, I/l and Ə ə Ğ ğ İ ı). Everything else uses `font-sans` (Inter).

### 8. Inertia, not a SPA router

- Links are Inertia `<Link>` (except the Google buttons, rule 4); visits are `router.get/put/...` with `preserveScroll` where the page stays.
- Shared state comes from Inertia props (`usePage().props`: `auth`, `locale`, `locales`, `translations`, `sidebarOpen`), not a client store. No i18next, axios, zustand, TanStack Query or react-hook-form.
- Routes are Wayfinder functions (`@/routes/*`, `@/actions/*`), never hand-written URLs.

### 9. Nothing leaves the host

- No CDN, external font, analytics, tracker or error service. Fonts and pictures are files in the repository; the font loader is `local()` in `vite.config.ts`.
- Pictures and sounds arrive as URLs in the page props (an avatar is `{ key, url }`); TypeScript never builds an asset path. A student's avatar is drawn by `StudentAvatar`.
- Children's names appear only on the teacher's pages, the board and the share card's allowed fields (architecture §6, §7), never in a URL, a log or a broadcast.
- From C4 (ADR 031) a room page in the live voice opens one more connection — a WebSocket at `/rtc` and the media — to the instance's own voice server, on the app's host. The voice library (`livekit-client`, from npm) is bundled and served by the app as its own chunk; the page connects with an empty list of ICE servers, so no public STUN server is ever asked, and nothing of it is fetched from elsewhere. No other page loads it.

### 10. The welcome page

- `/` (`WelcomeController`, page `welcome`) is the landing page for a teacher who opens a shared link, often on a phone. In this order it says what Elifba is, how a lesson goes, what a teacher gets, what a parent does at home (R7), what children's data it keeps, the adults' rooms (B7, only while the rooms switch is on) and what it costs (nothing), then shows the way in. A guest and a signed-in account see the same page; only the actions differ («Qeydiyyat» and «Daxil ol», or the account's own page through `/start`: «Siniflər» in the teacher area, «Uşaqlarım» in the parent area, «Otaqlar» in the player area, «Rolunuzu seçin» before the role choice).
- Its facts come from the server as props and are never retyped in `lang/` or TSX: the mechanics (`Mechanic` cases, named by `games.mechanics.*`), the packs (title in the interface language and palette, `ContentRepository`), the packs a child who cannot read yet plays (`picturePacks`: the keys of the packs that list a unit `Unit::reads()` is false for), the alphabet (`Alphabet`), a few avatars (`Avatars`) and the repository's address (`elifba.source_url`). It has no testimonials, school logos, user or star counts and no ratings.
- **The wall** at the top plays real rounds: `Play\Support\SampleRounds` builds a few `find` rounds at the first letter level with the real builder, with every letter learned and one built-in pack. The pack and the letters change with the Baku date. A visitor taps an item on the wall or the same item on the phone beside it (below it on a phone), the two ways a class plays. The wall is always light, like the board, with its colours from `game/palette.ts`, and Phaser is not loaded. It plays one round by itself after the page loads, which is the page's only motion that nobody triggered. With `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not, and a hit changes the item without animation.
- Type and colour follow rule 7 with one addition. Inter is for the teacher. Andika is only for what a child reads on the wall and the phone and for letters shown as letters (the alphabet). Colour comes from the tokens, the logo's green (`#15803D`) for the main action and the step numbers, and the packs' palettes.
- **«Evdə, uşağınızla»** (R7) follows «Nə əldə edirsiniz»: the teacher's story stays first and whole, and the parent's is an addition, not a second page. A plain link under the hero's actions («Valideynsiniz? Evdə uşağınızla da oynayın») jumps to it. It has the heading and one sentence on the left; a home game on the right (the board of a picture round with the parent's remote over its corner, as in step 3); three points, not numbered and marked by a green rule (choose «Valideyn», add the child, play together; the adult reads the prompt aloud); the picture games with the `picturePacks` as pills; the reading games at home; and that a child collects stars and is never compared (ADR 011: no ranking, place, podium or winners at home).
- **«Böyüklər üçün oyunlar»** (B7, G3; architecture §12, ADR 019 decision 4, ADR 026 decision 4) is drawn only while `RoomsArea::enabled()`: with the switch off the page gets no `rooms` prop and is what it was. It stands after «Uşaqlar haqqında nə saxlanılır» and before «Pulsuz və açıq mənbəli», so the teacher's and the parent's stories and the children's data come first and whole, and nothing of it stands among them: no link from the hero, no picture of a child's game. Its layout is «Evdə, uşağınızla»'s: on the left the heading, one sentence on what a room is and three facts from props — at most `elifba.rooms.max_players` people in a room, `elifba.rooms.rounds.min`–`.max` rounds a game, the difficulties of `content/rooms/levels.json` by name («Asan · Orta · Çətin · Çox çətin»); on the right the host's screen in a game with a player's phone over its corner (screenshots with made-up adult names, `resources/images/welcome/rooms-*.webp`); under them the three families, not numbered and each marked by a green rule, with their games — the family named by `rooms.setup.families.*`, a game by `rooms.mechanics.*.name` and `.line`, from the prop `rooms.families` (the `RoomFamily` cases, each with those of its `RoomMechanic` cases that have levels); then the way in — take «Oyunçu» at `/role` or in Settings → «Rollar», then «Oyun seç» (`/rooms`) or «Oyuna qoşul» (`/oyun`), which a visitor and an account without the role reach through the login, the registration and the role (architecture §12 "An invited account") — and one line on what other players see, with a link to `/privacy#rooms`. It lives in `components/welcome/rooms-section.tsx`, which imports nothing of `@/rooms` or `@/components/rooms` (`RoomsApartTest`): a game is named, never drawn with a room's components. It promises no benefit to memory, the mind or health (`RoomsWordingTest`). An account in the player area finds «Oyunlara keçin» where a teacher finds «Siniflərinizə keçin».
- «Dərs necə keçir» is a sequence and the only numbered part. Its pictures are screenshots of the app with demo data and made-up names, kept in `resources/images/welcome/` as WebP. They are imported through Vite and get `width`, `height` and an `alt` in both languages, plus `loading="lazy"` below the fold.
- The GitHub button is a plain link to `elifba.source_url` with GitHub's mark as an inline SVG (`components/github-link.tsx`, architecture §8 "Brand marks"). There is no GitHub script, badge, iframe or star count.
- `resources/views/app.blade.php` prints the description, Open Graph and Twitter tags on every page from `welcome.meta.*` (via `__()`, in the page's locale). A link shared on WhatsApp then shows a title, one sentence and `public/images/og.png` (1200×630, an absolute URL on the app's own host).

### 11. The install banner

- Chrome offers to install Elifba as an app when the manifest qualifies (architecture §10 "Installing Elifba"). An inline script in `app.blade.php` catches `beforeinstallprompt` before React mounts, holds back Chrome's own install bar on every page (the board and the remote included) and keeps the event on `window.elifbaInstall`; `appinstalled` clears it. Elifba's own banner, `InstallBanner` (`components/install-banner.tsx`), makes the offer.
- **Look.** A card: the logo's tile, «Elifba-nı quraşdırın», one line on what it gives (its own window, one tap from the home screen or the desktop), «Quraşdır» (the default `Button`; it calls the event's `prompt()`, which opens Chrome's own install dialog) and «İndi yox» (`ghost`). Tokens only (`bg-popover`, `text-muted-foreground`), so it follows dark mode; it slides in only without `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Place.** It floats (`fixed`, `z-40`: over the page and the bottom tab bar, under dialogs), so nothing moves when it comes or goes: across the bottom of a phone with the 16px gutter, bottom right from `sm` (384px wide), clear of the safe areas, and in the teacher shell below `lg` above the bottom tab bar.
- **Access.** A `section` named by its heading and described by its line, so screen readers list it as a region; it is reached by Tab after the page's content and never takes the focus. Esc inside it equals «İndi yox».
- **Where.** The welcome page, the auth pages (`AuthSimpleLayout`) and the shell of any area (`AppSidebarLayout`) each mount it once; `bannerAllowedOn()` in `lib/install-banner.ts` lists the pages that may show it, and Vitest checks the list against every page in `pages/`. Never the board (`games/board`, `lovhe`), the remote (`games/remote`, `pult/*`), the podium (made for the projector), `privacy`, `credits` or the share card; in the player area only «Otaqlar» (`rooms/index`, rule 15). A new page shows nothing until it is listed.
- **When.** Chrome has an unused offer, the app was not installed during the visit, the page does not run installed (`display-mode: standalone`, `window-controls-overlay` or `minimal-ui`) and «İndi yox» was not chosen in this browser in the last 14 days. The time of «İndi yox» is the one `localStorage` key `elifba.install-banner.dismissed-at`, read and written inside `try/catch` (without storage it holds until the page is reloaded). Cancelling Chrome's dialog counts as «İndi yox». An offer is used once; Chrome makes a new one on a later visit. Safari and Firefox never make one, so they show nothing (an iPhone adds Elifba from Share → Add to Home Screen, with `apple-touch-icon.png`).
- These rules live once, in the pure `showInstallBanner()` (`lib/install-banner.ts`, covered by Vitest); `hooks/use-install-offer.ts` only feeds it the browser's state and runs the two buttons.

### 12. The parent shell

The parent area (R4, architecture §1 "Accounts and roles", §4 "At home", §5 "A child's stars", ADR 011) is the same `AppLayout` with the parent's navigation (Uşaqlarım and Ayarlar), made for a parent holding a phone with a child of three to six beside them: few steps, big targets, nothing to compare.

- **Few steps.** From «Uşaqlarım» a game is two taps away: «Oyna» on the child's card, then «Başlat» on «Hazır oyun» at the top of the catalog, which plays the first game that can start with the child's remembered choices (rule 13); any other game is one tap more, its card. A game page's start bar sticks to the bottom of the screen, so a phone never scrolls to it.
- **Big targets.** Every control a parent taps is at least 44px (`h-11`, `size-11`); «Oyna» is 48px on a card and 56px on the child's page; an avatar to choose is a whole grid cell. Nothing depends on hover.
- **«Uşaqlarım»** (`children/index`): one card per child (`ChildCard`: the avatar, the name with `NameVoiceMark` when the board says it (rule 4), today's stars only, a link to the child's page and «Oyna»), one column on a phone and two or three from `sm`/`lg`, in the parent's own order. «Uşaq əlavə et» opens `AddChildDialog`: the first name (`ChildNameField`) and the avatar (`AvatarPicker`), with the first avatar none of the children has already chosen; the server opens the new child's page. «Sıranı dəyiş» (two children or more) turns the cards into a list with up and down (`ChildOrder`), saved at each move. At the limit (`limits.max`) the page says so instead of offering the dialog.
- **The child's page** (`children/show`): a card with the avatar, the name (with `NameVoiceMark`, or the line that the name list does not have it; rule 4), the edit button (`EditChildDialog`: name and avatar) and «Oyna»; then the child's stars (`ChildStars`: today, this week and in all as three tiles, the last seven days as rows with the server's date label, a bar and the number, and the retention note); the sticker album (`ChildStickers`, `lib/child-stickers.ts`: 12 animal stickers unlocked every 3 stars from `stars.total`, derived without extra database columns); the learned letters (`LearnedLetters`, shared with the class page, with an amber star badge on letters the child has solved in letter rounds via `ChildStars::practicedLetters()`); and «Uşağı sil», which asks first in a dialog. A child is deleted, never archived.
- **The setup** of a home game is rule 13's catalog with `home`: its title names the child («Yeni oyun: :name»), the picture and number shelves come before the reading ones, and a new child starts in «Asan». The phone's guest board pages pass `home` for a child too.
- **The board and the phone at home** (§4 "At home"): `BoardChild` (the avatar, «Oynayır», the name, and `BoardProgress` showing round dots and a streak badge when `streak >= 3`, with a celebratory avatar bounce when a round is solved) stands where the class's student button, «Püşk» (`randomStudent`, picking fairly among students who have not played yet in the current game) and «Növbəti» would, on the board's footer and on the remote, and is not a button. After 2 wrong taps in a round (`HINT_AFTER_MISSES = 2`), the board gently pulses the next target item (`hintItem()`). The start screen shows the child's avatar; the pairing card does not open by itself (`pairingOnLoad()` in `lib/game-players.ts`), and «Pultu qoş» on the start screen and in the header brings it. The result screen heads with the child's avatar (beside the total below `lg`), adds «:name artıq cəmi :count ulduz toplayıb!» — the stars before this game (`child.stars`) plus this game's — and shows a «Yeni stiker açıldı!» banner when the game's stars cross a 3-star sticker threshold.
- **Nothing to compare.** No ranking, place, podium, «best», total beside a sibling's or list ordered by stars appears anywhere in the parent area; `ChildPagesTest` checks the props of every parent page for it. Stars are amber (`fill-amber-400 text-amber-500`) on the parent's pages and the pack's accent on the board.

### 13. The game setup

«Yeni oyun» is a catalog of ready games, not a form of questions. The owner chose it on 2026-11-13 from three clickable prototypes (variant B, shelves like a game store), added «Asan / Normal» and remembered choices, and asked that a card look like its game. It replaces the three numbered questions chosen on 2026-11-11, which felt like an exam. «Son oyunlar» stays out (the owner took it out on 2026-11-11 because it muddled the page). `GameCatalog` (`components/games/game-catalog.tsx`, replacing `game-setup-form.tsx`, `setup-tiles.tsx` and `setup-start.tsx`) is the one game setup, for a class and for a child (`home`), on the setup page (`games/setup`: `/classes/{class}/games/create`, `/children/{child}/games/create`) and on the phone's guest board pages (`pult/guest-board`). It orders and explains the server's catalog (`GameSetupOptions`, architecture §2 "Game setup"): what can start is always the server's answer (`options[].startable`, the same check as `GameSetupData::rules()`), never a rule of the catalog's own. The choosing logic lives in `lib/setup-choices.ts` as pure functions with Vitest tests: which cards a mode shows, the shelf order, a card's levels and its default level, the remembered choices that still apply and the reason shown. The components only render it: `game-card.tsx` (the card), `game-scene.tsx` (its picture), `game-sheet.tsx` (a card's game page), `setup-extras.tsx` («Əlavə», with the row button and label `setup-tiles.tsx` had) and `game-icons.ts` (the unit and mode icons); «Sözlərim» is `components/words/my-words-dialog.tsx`, built from the words page's own components.

- **A card is a game.** One card per theme and game: a pack (`packs[]`) with a (unit, mechanic) the content offers it for. Its name comes from the server (`cards[].title`, architecture §2: «Qatarla cümlə qur», «Meyvələrlə say», «Heyvanlarla şəkil tap»); TypeScript never composes or suffixes it. A card covers every level of its unit that lists its mechanic; the level is chosen on the card's page.
- **«Asan» and «Normal».** Under the heading, a mode switch in a `bg-muted` track: two 58px buttons with `aria-pressed`, each a 38px icon square (`MousePointerClick`, `Puzzle`; `bg-primary` when chosen), the name and a muted line: «Asan» / «Yalnız vur» and «Normal» / «Tap, qur, say» (`games.setup.modes.*`, `.mode_hints.*`). «Asan» shows only the `pop` games: the child taps anything and nothing is wrong. «Normal» shows the rest: `find`, the three build games and `count`, where the board asks and the child answers. Under the switch, muted: «Rejim və seçimlər :name üçün yadda qalır.» (`games.setup.remembered`; `:name` the class's name or the child's first name). A tap switches at once and is saved at once (below). A new class starts in «Normal», a new child in «Asan».
- **The shelves.** One `section` per unit that has a card in the mode, in group order: the reading units (letter, syllable, word, sentence) first for a class, the picture and number units first at home; within a group, the content's order. «Cümlələr» has no shelf in «Asan» (a sentence has no `pop`). A shelf heads with a 40px `bg-muted` icon square (letter `CaseSensitive`, syllable `Blocks`, word `WholeWord`, sentence `Text`, picture `Image`, number `Hash`), the unit's name (`games.setup.units.*`) as an `h2` and a muted line «:hint · :count oyun» (`games.setup.shelf`, with `unit_hints.*`). Its cards are one row that scrolls sideways with `scroll-snap`, bleeding to the page's edges: 152px cards below 700px of content width, 186px from there. Within a shelf the cards follow the game order (the level's mechanics, `pop` first) and then the packs' order, with every card that can start before every locked one. Under the first reading shelf's heading stands the learned-letters line: «Oxu oyunlarında yalnız öyrənilən :count hərf işlənir:» and the letters in `font-board`, spaced, on a line of their own, or «Oxu oyunlarında bütün hərflər işlənir.» when `allLetters`, then «Dəyiş», an Inertia `Link` to `#learned-letters` on the class page or the child's page (on the guest board, no link).
- **Skill chips.** Above the shelves, one chip per shelf (its icon and name, 40px, a row that scrolls sideways) jumps to that shelf and moves the focus to its heading. Chips do not filter, and nothing is pressed.
- **«Hazır oyun».** Above the shelves, one large card: the first card that can start, in page order, with its remembered choices. It shows its scene at 4:3, a pill «Hazır oyun» (`games.setup.ready`), the card's name (22px, 26px beside the scene, bold), a muted summary line (below), «Başlat» (the default `Button`, 52px, full width, at most 260px beside the scene; «Lövhədə başla» on the guest board) and a link «Seçimlər» (`games.setup.options`) that opens its game page, as a tap on its scene does. When the catalog is at least 700px wide (a container query: the setup page from `lg`, never the phone's guest board) the scene is a 340px column beside the text. Its background is `palette[0]` at 12% over `bg-background`. So «Oyna» → «Başlat» is still two taps (rule 12), and any other game three: «Oyna», the card, «Başlat». When nothing can start at all, `games.setup.none` and the learned-letters link replace «Hazır oyun» and the shelves.
- **The card** (`GameCard`): a `button` whose accessible name is the card's name: the scene (1:1, `rounded-[22px]`), then the name (14.5px, semibold, balanced) and one muted line, «:count səviyyə» (`games.setup.level_count`) when more than one of its levels can start, else that level's `hint`. No border and no box around the text, as in a store. With `hover:hover` the scene lifts by 2px; with `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves.
- **The scene** (`GameScene`, `aria-hidden`) shows the game before it is played. It is drawn like the board, always light whatever the theme, from the pack's palette and its cover only (no new art, ADR 014): a sky from `palette[0]` mixed with white, a sun circle in `palette[2]` at the top right, a hill in `palette[1]` along the bottom (a palette with fewer colours repeats its first), and the cover with a soft drop shadow. Over it, the card's `sample` (architecture §2) as the board would show it, in `font-board` on white with a shadow in `palette[0]`: for `pop` and `find`, the cover at the lower left and three round bubbles beside it; `find` rings the middle one and puts a `Search` badge on it, `pop` puts a `MousePointerClick` badge on the first. For a build game, the cover above a row of tiles: the first tile filled, the rest dashed empty slots. For `count`, three small copies of the cover above three digit bubbles, the middle ringed. At a picture level, the cover alone and large, ringed for `find`. Badges and rings are `palette[0]` mixed with the board's ink, never black. Sizes are container query units (`cqw`), so the same scene draws a 152px card, «Hazır oyun» and the game page.
- **The game page** (`GameSheet`). A tap on a card opens it: below `lg` a full-screen `Dialog` over the catalog, from `lg` the same `Dialog` as a 390px panel on the right, like a sheet (so its own round button is its only close control); the catalog stays where it was. From top to bottom: the scene at 4:3, full width, with a round 42px button over its corner («Oyunlara qayıt» with `ChevronLeft` below `lg`, «Bağla» with `X` from `lg`; Esc closes); two pills (the unit's icon and name; the mode's icon and «Asan» or «Normal»); the card's name as the dialog's title (24px, bold); the mechanic's line (`games.setup.mechanic_hints.*`, `pop_say` at a level that does not read); **«Səviyyə»** when two of the card's levels exist: two option cards in a row (`aria-pressed`, the level's title over its `hint`, the chosen one with a `border-primary` ring on `bg-accent`); with one level, its hint as a muted line; **«Əlavə»** (below); the muted line ««Başlat»dan sonra bu seçimlər :name üçün yadda qalır.» (`games.setup.remembered_after_start`); and the start bar, sticky at the bottom with the safe-area padding: the summary line, centred, over «Başlat» (52px, full width; «Lövhədə başla» on the guest board) with `Spinner` while processing. The summary is the level's title followed, where they apply, by «böyük hərflər» or «kiçik hərflər», «:count hərf» and «yalnız mənim sözlərim», joined with « · » (`games.setup.summary.*`).
- **«Əlavə».** As before: a block closed by default, a 52px button (`aria-expanded`) with «Əlavə» and, muted, its values («böyük hərflər · bütün hərflər»); it opens by itself when the server returns an error on one of its fields; a game whose unit does not read has none. Inside, in this order: «Hərflərin yazılışı» (reading units), two buttons, each the first three learned letters in `font-board` over «Böyük hərflər» / «Kiçik hərflər»; «Hansı hərflər soruşulsun?» (the letter level only), «Hamısı (:count)» first, then one 44px toggle per learned letter, with «Az hərf seçsəniz, yanlarına oxşar hərflər əlavə olunur.»; «Hansı sözlər?» (only where `own` is not null): when `own.startable`, «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» and «Hamısı»; otherwise, in the teacher area only, `no_own_words` is one muted line with a link to «Sözlər» and any other reason is that reason with «Söz əlavə et» (an `outline` button with `Plus`), which opens «Sözlərim» (below; its «Bu sözlərlə oyna» turns «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» on on this page); the parent area shows nothing then.

On a teacher's catalog (never `home`, so never a parent's), each card whose game can play the teacher's words at one of its levels (`own` is not null on one of its options, `takesOwnWords()`: the syllable and word games but «Söz yığ») has a round 44px button over its scene's top-left corner: white like the game page's round button, `Plus`, `aria-label` «Söz əlavə et» (`words.add.title`) and described by the card's name. - **Remembered choices** (the owner, 2026-11-13: a choice made once is kept). Each class and each child has its own (architecture §2 "Remembered setup"): the mode, the level per unit, the letter case per unit, the chosen letters and «Yalnız mənim sözlərim». The mode is saved when it is tapped; the choices on a game page are saved when that game starts, so what is kept is what was played («Hamısı» only when «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» could have played instead, so an owner's first words still open with it on). A game page opens with the remembered level of its unit when that level can start with this card, else the first that can; the remembered letter case of its unit, else the server's default (capitals at the letter level, as written otherwise); the remembered letters still learned (none when fewer than two are left); the remembered «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» where it can start, else on where it can start. Closing a game page without starting forgets what was changed on it. The guest board uses the chosen players' choices and saves them the same way.
- **Locked.** A game the content does not offer for a pack has no card. A card whose options exist but none can start (`startable` false: too few learned letters, words the owner hid leave too few, or the board cannot draw it yet) is shown locked, never hidden: its scene in greyscale at 45% opacity, a 30px white `Lock` badge over its top right corner, its name in `text-muted-foreground` and its line «Daha çox hərf lazımdır» (`games.setup.locked.letters`) for a learned-letters shortage (`too_few_letters`, `too_few_syllables`, `too_few_words`, `too_few_sentences`), else the reason, or «Tezliklə» when `drawable` is false. It is `aria-disabled` but focusable, and a tap opens its game page with «Bu oyun hələ açılmır.» (`games.setup.locked.game`), the full reason of its first level and «Hərfləri dəyiş» (the setup page only), and «Başlat» disabled. On a game page that can start, a locked level is a dashed option card with `Lock` and «Daha çox hərf lazımdır», never chosen; a tap shows its full reason and «Hərfləri dəyiş» under the row.
- **Look.** Tokens only outside the scene, so dark mode follows (rule 7); the scene stays light like the board. Every choice is a `button type="button"`, with `aria-pressed` where it toggles; every target is at least 44px and nothing depends on hover. The game page posts `mechanic`, `pack`, `level`, `letter_case`, `letters[]` and `own_words` (no `rounds`: the server's default); server errors show with `InputError` on the game page, and an error on a hidden field (the class or the child, on the guest board) at the top.
- **The guest board** (`pult/guest-board`): «Gedən oyunlar» names each live game «:players» over «:level · :mechanic · :status» (`games.setup.summary.game`). «Yeni oyun» first asks «Kim oynayır?» (one 44px button per child, then per class, the first chosen), then shows the catalog for those players: mode switch, «Hazır oyun», shelves and game pages, with «Lövhədə başla».
- **Entry points.** On the class page «Oyun başlat» is the main action (the first header button, the only `default` one, on a full-width row of its own below `sm`). `LearnedLetters` (class page and child's page) has `id="learned-letters"` and a `scroll-mt` of the sticky header's height. «Uşaqlarım» and the child's page keep «Oyna» (rule 12).
- **The board's start screen** (not built yet) is to say the card's name: as its title `cards[].title` of the game («Qatarla cümlə qur»), above it who plays, under it «:level · :count raund», so the adult and the child see one name from the card to the wall. `BoardProps` is to carry that title (architecture §2).
- **Words.** Every string above is a key in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json` (rule 2). New: `games.names.{unit}_{mechanic}`, the fourteen games' names: «Hərf vur», «Hərf tap», «Heca vur», «Heca tap», «Heca qur», «Söz vur», «Söz tap», «Söz yığ», «Cümlə qur», «Şəkil vur», «Şəkil tap», «Rəqəm vur», «Rəqəm tap», «Say» ("Tap letters", "Find the letter", "Tap syllables", "Find the syllable", "Build a syllable", "Tap words", "Find the word", "Build the word", "Build a sentence", "Tap pictures", "Find the picture", "Tap numbers", "Find the number", "Count"); `games.titles.{unit}_{mechanic}`, the card names with the pack's `:with` («:with hərf tap», ":game :with" in English: "Find the letter :with"); `games.setup.mode`, `.modes.{easy,normal}`, `.mode_hints.{easy,normal}`, `.remembered`, `.remembered_after_start`, `.ready`, `.options`, `.level_count`, `.shelf`, `.skills` (the chips' group label «Bacarıqlar»), `.back` («Oyunlara qayıt») and `.locked.game`. The owner's choices of 2026-11-13, on every board (the teacher board, the home board and the guest board alike, unless said otherwise); the server's side is architecture §2 "Game setup" («Yenidən oyna») and §4 "Round count".

### 15. The player shell

The player area (B2–B7, architecture §12, ADRs 019–027) is the same `AppLayout` with the player's navigation (Oyunlar, Keçmiş oyunlar and Ayarlar since G3), made for an adult holding a phone in a room with others: one thing to do at a time, a round that fits the screen, a device that never judges, scores or keeps the time, and a game whose every moment moves, and sounds where it places a sound, as ADR 025 designs it. The screen (`rooms/screen`) is without the shell, and from F2 so is a room's page (`rooms/show`), inside its own frame ("The game frame" below, ADR 032). Its parts are in `components/rooms/` and only there: no room file imports the children's side (`@/game`, `@/components/{games,children,classes,students,rankings,share,words}`), and no other file imports `@/rooms` or `@/components/rooms` (`RoomsApartTest`).

- **Phone first.** 375px wide is the design width; every page works there without sideways scroll (long names truncate). Tokens only, so both themes follow (rule 7), with the room's own colours below.
- **Asking first.** Closing the room, ending a game, removing a player, deleting a room and taking one's name out ask in `RoomConfirm` (`components/rooms/room-confirm.tsx`: the shared `ConfirmDialog`'s look with 44px buttons, a Wayfinder form or a callback for the `fetch` routes). - **«Otaqlar»** (`rooms/index`). The title and one line; then a card for each open room the account is in — its code large, «:count oyunçu», «Siz açmısınız» for the host, and «Otağa keç» (52px) — since that is where it goes next; then «Otağa qoşul», the six-digit code field (`inputMode="numeric"`, 56px, wide tracking; the server reads it, spaces and dashes dropped) with «Davam et»; then «Otaq aç» with «Mən də oynayıram» (a 44px checkbox row, ticked) and one line on what the host does; it is not offered while the account hosts an open room. «Keçmiş otaqlar» lists the past rooms, newest first, each a link card: the day (`DateLabel`), its games as quiet chips («:game · :difficulty»), and one muted line of the players' count, «Siz açmısınız» and the account's own place and points («Yeriniz: 2-ci · 37 xal», the label from the server) or «Xal qazanmamısınız».
- **The entrance** (`rooms/join`). At `/otaq` the code field alone, focused, with «Davam et»; a code that opens no room is refused under the field («Bu kodla açıq otaq tapılmadı. Kodu yoxlayıb yenidən yazın.», the same sentence whatever the reason, architecture §12 "Rooms and joining"), on «Otaqlar» too when it was typed there, and the typed code stays in the field — never an error page; ten tries a minute, then «Çox cəhd etdiniz. Bir dəqiqədən sonra yenidən yoxlayın.» in the same place. At `/otaq/{code}` the code in large tabular figures, «Otaqda adınız belə görünəcək:» over the account's name as a card with a 44px «Dəyiş» link to Settings → Profil, the server's refusal («Bu otaq kilidlidir.», «Bu otaq doludur.») and «Qoşul» (56px, full width), with nothing about the room beyond its code.
- **The room page** (`rooms/show`) follows the state (`useLiveRoom()`) and draws what it says; it never derives a phase, a solution or a point. Outside a game its header has «Otaq» and «Kilidli» while the room is locked; during a game the title is for screen readers only and the round's head carries the code as a chip (`CodeChip`, 44px) that opens the code and a 224px QR in a dialog, so a late player can scan it from any phone in the room. By the state:
    - **The lobby** (no game yet, or a game over): the code card (`CodeCard`) — the QR of `join.url` (120px, `qrcode.react`, dark on a white tile in both themes, `RoomQr`) beside «Otaq kodu», the code at 32px in two groups of three, and «QR kodu skan edin və ya :address ünvanında kodu yazın» with `join.entry_url` without its scheme; then, after a game, «Oyun bitdi» with the game's name and the final (below); then «Oyunçular (:count)», one 48px row each: the name, «siz» on the viewer's own (tinted `bg-accent`), «otağı açan» on the host's, «çıxıb» on one who left, and for the host «Çıxar» (asks first, naming the player); then «Otağın idarəsi», the host's tools: «Kilidlə» / «Kilidi aç» (the label says what a tap does, with `Lock` / `LockOpen`), «Böyük ekran» (a link, with one muted line: open it on a laptop or a television signed in to the same account) and «Otağı bağla» (destructive, asks first); a player's «Otaqdan çıx» last, quiet. The host's «Yeni oyun» is the `RoomActionBar`; a player's bar is a waiting line instead («Oyunun başlamasını gözləyin.», with `Hourglass`).
    - **The setup** (the host's «Yeni oyun»): a dialog, full screen below `sm`. The games grouped by family under the family's name («Yaddaş»), each a card (`aria-pressed`) with a small drawing of its round in tokens (a row of tiles, a grid with lit cells, a rising row with a dashed place, three alike and one apart, tiles under dashed places, four lines with one stronger), its name and its line; «Çətinlik» as four 44px segments («Asan»…«Çox çətin») over one muted line of what the level holds, from the server's `levels` («5–6 element · 4,5 san. baxmaq · 15 san. cavab», «4 × 4 cədvəl, 5–6 xana · …»; a logic level «5 ədəd, 1–300 · ədədi silsilə, həndəsi silsilə, kvadratlar · 4 variant · baxmaq yoxdur · 20 san. cavab», «5 variant · sözlər · baxmaq yoxdur · 12 san. cavab»); «Raund sayı» as one 44px button per step (every multiple of 5 from the server's `rounds.min` to `.max`, and the two ends), never a typed number. The dialog's bottom bar holds the summary («:game · :difficulty · :count raund») over «Oyunu başlat» (52px, `Spinner` while starting); its own round ✕ is 44px. The rounds start at the minimum, as B3 had it; the page keeps the last choice while it is open, so the next «Yeni oyun» opens on it.
    - **A round** (`LiveRound`) replaces the lobby while a game is live and fits a 375 × 667 screen with the shell around it (from F2 in the game frame, without the shell: "The game frame"). Its head (`RoundHead`): «Raund :index / :total» — in the reveal «Raund :index / :total bitdi», so the head says in words that the round is over — over the game's name and difficulty, muted, with the sound toggle (a 44px round icon, «Oyun səsləri» to screen readers, "Sound" below) and the code chip beside them; under them the time bar (below) with the seconds left beside it. Then one line saying what to do — the mechanic's `study` or `ask` text — and the round. The countdown is «Oyun başlayır» over the seconds to the first round at 72px in a 144px ring, then the game's name and the mechanic's line. The study is drawn only from a state in `study` and only while the estimated server clock is before `answer_from_ms`; then, until the question arrives, the study's place stays with the material covered — a row's tiles face down, a grid all dark — never an empty page. The question's items are buttons: 60px tiles; a grid's cells as wide as the page allows and no taller than the screen leaves (`100svh − 22rem`), never under 44px. Under them, while taps are being made, one 44px row: «Geri» (a sequence) or «Seçilib: :count / :total» (a set) beside «Cavab verib: :count / :total»; once the answer is sent that row gives way to its note — «Göndərilir…», «Cavabınız qəbul olundu.», «Vaxt bitmişdi…» — with the count of answers under it, or to «Cavab göndərilmədi» with «Yenidən göndər». The host's «Oyunu bitir» (`CircleStop`) is a quiet button at the end, asking first. A host who does not play sees the round without buttons and «Siz bu oyunda oynamırsınız».
    - **Taps.** A `sequence` (`recall_order`): each tap puts the item into the next of a row of slots above the items, the tapped item dims with its number, and «Geri» takes back the last. A `set` (`recall_cells`): a tapped cell turns the viewer's colour, and a second tap takes it back. A `choice` (the logic games, B5): the options are buttons of one size in the server's order — two by two when they pair up, one column when they do not (`optionColumns()`), so none stands apart — and one tap chooses: the chosen option takes the viewer's colour, the others fade and stop taking taps, and the note says the answer went; `next_in_sequence` draws its row (`ask.shown`) above them on one line, sized to its container, with a dashed «?» in the next place. The word games (B6): «Anaqram» is a `sequence` of letter tiles (60px, `text-3xl` semibold) in the server's order and case, shared into even rows of at most five (`tileRows()` in `mechanics/word-layout.ts`: eight letters stand four over four), under a row of the word's places (up to 44px wide each, 48px high); a tap moves the letter into the next place — the next empty place has a thicker dashed border in `primary` — and leaves a dashed gap where its tile stood, and «Geri» takes back the last. «Düzgün yazılış» is a `choice` whose four spellings stand in one column, full width (56px high, 28px semibold, `tracking-wide`), so «ı» and «i», «ə» and «e», «ş» and «s» read large and letter under letter; its options are otherwise B5's. Either way the answer is sent by the tap that makes `ask.pick` items, with no «Göndər» button: one attempt, all or nothing, and the clock rewards the quick. After it nothing can be tapped.
    - **The reveal**: the viewer's verdict first, from `reveal.mine` only — «Düzdür» with `Check` or «Səhvdir» with `X`, the points («+17 xal») and the time («2,4 saniyəyə», `Intl.NumberFormat` in the page's locale) — or «Bu raunda cavab vermədiniz»; then the mechanic's solution with the viewer's own answer beside it, drawn as two rows or two marks and never compared item by item on the device — for a `choice`, the options again in their places, the right one green with «Düzgün» (`Check`) and the viewer's own with «Sizin» (`Check` or `X`, coloured by `mine.correct`), the rest faded, and in `next_in_sequence` the right number written into the row's «?»; in «Xanaları yadda saxla» the grid under «Düzgün xanalar» (`Check`, emerald, semibold): the cells that were lit green (an `emerald` tint and border) with a `Check` each, the others only a dashed outline, and the viewer's own taps ringed in `primary` («sizin toxunduğunuz xanalar») — never the study's amber lamp, so a player who did not answer cannot take the reveal for the next round's cells to remember (the owner's report from elifba.io, 2026-11-18); in «Ardıcıllığı yadda saxla» the right order in green tiles under «Düzgün ardıcıllıq» with `Check`, and the viewer's own row under it in the plain tiles; in «Anaqram» the round's word in green cells under «Söz» (from `reveal.solution`, as the tiles write it) and, under it, the viewer's word only when it is another — green with «Sizin · bu söz də düzgündür» when the server took it (`mine.correct`), so a second word the letters spell is never drawn as a mistake, red with «Sizin» when it did not; the same word is one row marked «Düzgün · sizin»; in «Düzgün yazılış» the four spellings again in their column with B5's marks; then «Sıralama»: the game's ranking with each player's result of the round (`Check` +points, `X` +0, «—» without an answer). The head says «Növbəti: :seconds san.», or «Yekun: :seconds san.» after the last round, over the bar of the wait.
- **The time bar** (`TimeBar`) is how much of the phase is left — the study, the answer, the wait to the next round — computed by `timeLeft()` in `resources/js/rooms/clock.ts` from the estimated server clock (`serverClock()`), never the device's; it is for show and decides nothing. It is a 8px rounded track (`bg-muted`) with a `bg-primary` fill scaled from the start, redrawn with the seconds every 200 ms with a linear transition (none with `prefers-reduced-motion`); in the last 3 s of an answer it turns `bg-destructive`, and the seconds beside it say the same in words, in `text-red-600` (`red-400` dark), each second with a small pulse.
- **Motion** (ADR 025). Every moment of a game has its movement, the same on the phone and the screen (larger there), started only by the state or the viewer's own tap and changing nothing about the time:
    - **The page's first moment is still.** `roomMoment()` (`components/rooms/motion.ts`, Vitest) keys a moment by the game, the round's index and its phase; the page draws the one it opened on — a reload, a late join, the screen opened mid-game — complete and still, and animates each later one, through `data-motion="on"` on the moment's block and the `room-motion:` variant (`resources/css/app.css`). Entrances are keyed on the moment, never on a render: a state fetched again replays nothing.
    - **Countdown**: each second's figure drops into its ring and the ring ripples. **Study**: the material comes in within 0.25 s (a row's tiles in their order, a grid's lit cells lighting) and is covered at once at `answer_from_ms`, never faded. **Question**: the line and then the items come in, the last within 0.2 s of the first. **Round**: «Raund :index / :total» pops when a round begins.
    - **Taps** are the viewer's own and move the same for every item: a tapped tile, cell or option pops as it takes the viewer's colour, a filled slot or place pops its item in, and the next empty place of an anagram breathes. **Sent**: the note rises in, its `Hourglass` turns while the room answers — one movement for every answer, from the receipt or `answered` only; the count of answers pops when it changes (on the screen the large count, its bar filling, and a `border-primary` card once everyone has answered).
    - **Reveal**: the verdict first, the same entrance for «Düzdür», «Səhvdir» and no answer, its points counting up from 0; then the solution, the right item popping (a right cell's `Check`; never the study's lamp switching on again); then the ranking, whose rows move from the order of the previous reveal the page showed (`rankShift()`, never a re-sort) while each total counts up from `points − result.points` to the server's `points` and the round's results pop in.
    - **Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves** (`motion-safe:` or `room-motion:` on every animation, a count shows its number, rows stand in place, no confetti): each moment looks as when the page is opened on it. Keyframes are `--animate-room-*` in `resources/css/app.css`, next to the welcome page's.
- **Sound** (ADR 025 "Sound" and its "As built"; the set, ADR 028). The screen is the room's speaker — of its cues; with live voice (ADR 031) voices are heard on the phones and never on the screen; a phone plays only quiet cues of its own, at half the screen's level — and in a room without a screen (the state's `room.screen` false; ADR 028 as amended 2026-11-19) every phone plays the room's cues as well — the winner's cheer included, its final's at a phone's pace — at its own level (`carriesRoom()`; ADR 030 as amended 2026-11-19); in a match the phone of the seat whose turn begins plays that moment's room cues and its own clock with a screen too, and vibrates once ("Board games" → "Sound", ADR 034 as amended 2026-11-21); each device has its own mute. When a cue plays is `roomCues()`'s (`components/rooms/sound-cues.ts`, Vitest), from two consecutive states and the estimated server clock, and only for a moment the page saw begin: a reload, a late join or a state fetched again plays nothing, and a moment heard more than 4 s after it began is joined silently.
    - **The screen** plays the countdown — one 3 s file, a beat on each second it shows, ending at `opens_ms` — and `go` as round 0 opens (in place of its study or its question); a later round's study and question opening; the clock — one 5 s file ending at `bell_ms`, its last 3 s the most urgent as the time bar turns red, the same whoever has answered — a bell when the round closes (the same whatever anyone answered: it has no viewer), the ranking coming in; in the final a hit as the third and the second places open, a roll that ends where the first place opens, then a fanfare and the winner's cheer (below); and between games `join` when a player appears (one for a state, never a name).
    - **Long cues and stops.** The countdown, the clock and the roll are placed on the server's clock (`ends`): a page that hears its moment late starts the file from its middle, and plays nothing with less than 250 ms of it left (`MIN_TAIL_MS`); the clock is heard 500 ms ahead (`LEAD_MS`) so it starts to the sample. Every cue stops when its moment ends — the round closed early, the game ended, a new one began — one scheduled is dropped and one sounding fades out within 40 ms (`STOP_MS`); a hidden page plays nothing and stops what sounds, and coming back plays nothing it missed. Its top bar holds the toggle before «Tam ekran»: «Oyun səslərini aç», filled (`default`), while the browser still waits for a gesture, then «Oyun səsləri» (`outline`, `Volume2` or `VolumeX`), `aria-pressed` when the sound is on, at least 44px. The first press turns the room's sound on, the next ones mute and unmute; any other gesture on the page also turns it on.
    - **A phone** plays its own tap (the same for every item), a tap taken back («Geri», a cell tapped again), its answer sent once the state says `answered` (one sound for every answer) and, from the reveal only, its verdict — never a room's moment. Its first tap anywhere turns sound on (at once where the page was reached by a tap, as after «Qoşul»). Its toggle is the round's head's round 44px icon (`aria-pressed`, `aria-label` «Səs»), beside the code chip.
    - **The winner's cheer** (ADR 030). After the fanfare (`winnerAt + FANFARE_MS`) the screen ends the game as a home board does: the first name of the account that holds the final's first place (the built-in name list's clip, «Nihat!»), then `PHRASE_GAP_MS` (150 ms) later «Əla oynadın!», both at `VOICE_GAIN` so they sit with the effects. Two or three who share the first place are said in the state's ranking order, `NAME_GAP_MS` apart, then «Əla oynadınız!»; four or more, «Əla oynadınız!» alone; a name off the list, or a clip that has not arrived `NAME_LEAD_MS` before its time, is skipped, and a phrase without a name is said alone. The clips are fetched only when the page hears the final begin and its sound is on — names from `rooms.members.voice` by ids, the phrases from the screen's `finish` prop (the screen passes `useRoomSound()` its `cheer`) — kept decoded in memory only, and stop with the moment like every cue. Nothing on the page shows that a name is said; a phone never fetches or says one.
    - **A taunt's voice** (T3; ADR 029 "Amended 2026-11-19 (T3)", decisions 44–48). The board's voice (Nigar) says a taunt's Azerbaijani phrase from its one clip (the prop `taunts[].voice`, made by `content:voice`) on the devices that draw its reaction — the target's phone and the screen — and nowhere else: never the thrower, never another phone, in a room without a screen only the target's phone. A `roomCues()` cue, `{sound: 'taunt', id, key}`, once for a taunt the page saw arrive (`tauntMoment()`), never for a page opened on it or a state fetched again; it starts with the reaction, is not cut by the round's close, stops when the page is hidden, the game ends or the room closes; one at a time, a second waiting and dropped if its reaction is over. At `SPEAKER_GAIN` × `VOICE_GAIN`; silenced by the device's mute, by the screen's «Atmacalar» with the reaction, and by the host's switch. The clips are fetched once sound is unlocked and on and the room's taunts are on; an untouched iPhone shows the reaction in silence. The same clip in every locale.
    - **Mute** is this device's choice: `localStorage` `elifba.rooms.screen.muted` / `elifba.rooms.phone.muted` (`rooms/sound.ts`, read and written in `try`/`catch`), never the account's: no setting, route or Settings → «Oyun» entry (ADR 019 decision 8). A muted device makes no audio at all; `prefers-reduced-motion` does not mute. Where audio is missing or refused, nothing throws and the game plays on in silence.
    - The files are the room's own (`content/rooms/sounds.json`, `content/assets/rooms/sounds/`, made by `scripts/room-sounds/make.py` from CC0 recordings and our synthesis), and a page gets only its own speaker's URLs in its props (`sounds`): never a children's sound, voice, speech, music or loop. A phone never plays the clock, even in a room played without a screen (ADR 028 "As chosen").
- **Right, wrong and waiting** are words with an icon — `Check`, `X`, `Hourglass` — never a colour alone. The room's own colours, beside the tokens: right `emerald-600` (`emerald-400` dark), wrong `red-600` (`red-400` dark; the dark `destructive` token is too dark for text) on a `destructive` tint, a lit cell `amber-400` (a lamp in both themes) — in the study only: the reveal draws the cells that were lit as the right answer, green — the viewer's taps `primary`.

A row is the name (truncated; «Oyunçu» for a name taken out), «siz» on the viewer's own row (tinted `bg-accent`), and the points in tabular figures. - **A closed room** (`rooms/show` with `games`) is its results: «Otaq bağlanıb» as a badge by the title; «Otağın sıralaması»; «Oyunlar (:count)», one card per game in order («:game · :difficulty · :count raund») whose ranking opens and closes under it (`aria-expanded`, the first open); then «Adımı sil» with its line («Adınız bu otağın nəticələrindən silinir; xallarınız «Oyunçu» adı ilə qalır.») and, for the host, «Otağı sil» (destructive). - **The screen** (`rooms/screen`) has no shell (`app.tsx`, like the board) and is made for a laptop or a television across a room: sizes grow with the viewport (`vh`), tokens follow the theme, the QR always on white. In a game the top bar also shows «Kod :code». In a game: the countdown, the study at its largest, the question as every player sees it (no taps) with the count of answers as a large «:count / :total» and a bar, the reveal with the solution beside the ranking with each player's result. After a game: «Oyun bitdi» and the final (above), with the code and a small QR in a corner for whoever comes next. Nothing on it writes; it holds no host tool.
- **The install banner** (rule 11) shows on `rooms/index` only: never on `rooms/join` (it would cover «Qoşul»), `rooms/show` (it would cover a round) or `rooms/screen`.

In the viewer's own turn the tiles `mine.legal` names are **lit** — raised 6px, at full strength — and the others are dimmed to 55 % and are no buttons (`aria-disabled`): raised **and** strong, never a colour alone. Outside its turn every tile stands level and takes no tap. A watcher and a host who does not sit have no hand: the table takes its place, with «Oyunu izləyirsiniz» in the line.
    - **Taps.** A tap on a lit tile that fits one end posts the play at once (`POST /rooms/{room}/acts`, `path` `[tile, end]`; a hand's first tile `[tile]`). A lit tile that fits both ends lifts (a `primary` ring) and the line on the table shows a dashed target at each end, where the next tile would lie (`layoutChain()`'s targets, in `--domino-target`), each a button at least 44px square however small the line is drawn, named «Sol uca · :number» and «Sağ uca · :number» for a screen reader; the line under the table says «Ucu seçin: zəncirdə hədəfə toxunun» with «Geri» beside it. A tap on a target posts `[tile, end]`; a tap on another lit tile starts again with it; «Geri» puts the tile back (ADR 038 decision 88: no «Sol uca» and «Sağ uca» buttons in the line). A posted tile stands at its end in the viewer's colour until the state answers; a refusal puts it back with «Daş qoyulmadı. Yenidən cəhd edin.», and the next tap clears the words. Which tiles are lit and which ends a tile may take is `taps.ts`'s, pure (Vitest), over `mine.legal` alone.
    - **The table** (`DominoTable`) is one part on the phone and the screen, smaller on the phone. It holds the line, the seats round it and the boneyard. **The line** is `line` from its left end to its right, each tile lying as the state says (`left`, `right`), a double across it — a drawing, as in the game itself, where a double opens nothing; the last tile played is framed in `primary`, and its two ends carry their open numbers as badges («5», «3») large enough to read where the tiles are small. The line is a snake from the table's top left («ilan», ADR 038 decision 87): along the first row from its left end, turning down at the edge and back along the next; one pure function of the line and the box (`layoutChain()` in `chain.ts`, Vitest) lays it out, the same on every device — a unit from 30px down to 9 on a phone, from 64 down to 24 on the screen, the largest at which the line and its two targets fit — shrinks every tile together when even the smallest would not fit, and stands the snake in the middle of the table; on a phone a tile of the line may be small, since the ends are named. A play at the left end moves the rest of the snake one place, drawn still. The table is the felt (`--domino-felt`, its vignette and grain); the tiles are ivory (`--domino-face`, `--domino-pip`, `--domino-pin`), and a seat's tiles face down are `--domino-back`. **The seats** stand round the table as chips: the viewer's own at the bottom edge, the others in the order of the turns clockwise — at pairs the partner opposite. A chip has the name (or `Bot` «Kompüter»), tile backs for the count of its tiles with the number («×5»), «para» on the viewer's partner, `Bot` beside the name of a seat that is played for («:name üçün kompüter oynayır» for screen readers), and while it is that seat's turn a ring in the side's colour with the seconds left, which turn the wrong colour with `Timer` in the last five. On a phone a chip is two lines, 36px tall — the mark, `Bot` and the name over «para», the back, «×5» and the seconds — so three chips across 375px keep their names; the line's box stands 46px inside the table's top and bottom edges. A chip is a named group («:name: :count daş») for a screen reader. **The boneyard** is a pile of backs with «Bazar · :count» at the tables that have one; when nobody may draw it says why in one line: «Son 2 daş çəkilmir» or «Uclara yalnız qoşa düşə bilər — bazar bağlıdır».
    - **What happened by itself** is drawn in order after the play that caused it (`last.then`), a step at a time, on every device: a seat that drew gets «Bazar :count×» on its chip and the pile's count and the chip's go down and up a step at a time — no tile travels from the pile (ADR 038 "As built (Y7)" d2.7); a seat that passed gets «PAS» with `Flag` on its chip for two seconds. The mark stands over a chip at the table's bottom edge and under one along its top edge (`markBelow()`), so the felt's edge never cuts it, and it is keyed by its moment (`stepKey()`), so a seat that passes again in the next turn shows «PAS» again. The phone whose own play caused the steps draws them too. On the viewer's own phone the drawn tiles (`mine.drawn`) arrive in the hand and the line under the table says it in a sentence: «Uyğun daşınız yox idi — bazardan :count daş götürdünüz», then «Sizin növbənizdir» — or «Uyğun daşınız yoxdur — pas». A turn the server played for a player says «Vaxt bitdi — :name üçün kompüter oynadı» in the line, on every device.
    - **The line under the table**: «Sizin növbənizdir» with `Hand` — at a hand's first tile under R6 «:tile ilə açın», for a seat with the right «İstədiyiniz daşla açın» —, «:name oynayır» with `Hourglass`, «Kompüter (Orta) oynayır» with `Bot`, «Oyunu izləyirsiniz» for a watcher, in the countdown «Oyun :seconds saniyəyə başlayır». At pairs the seat that may give the opening has a 44px `outline` «Tərəfdaşım açsın» beside its band, and after it the partner reads «Tərəfdaşınız açmağı sizə verdi». Words with an icon, as right and wrong are.
    - **Whose turn it is.** Whether it is the viewer's turn is one pure function of the state and the viewer (the phase is `play` and the viewer holds the seat to move), as `yourTurn()` is for a match. For that seat the line is the band — the 44px row filled `rival-left-deep`, `Hand` and the words white and semibold — which pops in once when a turn the page saw begin starts and stands still for a page opened on its turn and under `prefers-reduced-motion`; its own chip's ring runs; its phone plays `turn`, vibrates once (50 ms, under its mute, where the browser can), and its tab's title is «● Sizin növbənizdir». Every seat's phone holds a wake lock while its game is live (`useWakeLock()`). No notification, permission prompt or setting.
    - **Away.** A seat whose last two turns were played for it reads, in the hand's place over its dimmed tiles, one card: «Sizin yerinizə kompüter oynayır» and a filled 56px «Oyuna qayıt», which posts `back`; the page waits for the state. Everyone else sees `Bot` on its chip.
    - **The strip** (`RivalryStrip`, from `stripRows()`). At «2 nəfər» and «Para 2×2» the two sides: on a seat's phone its own on the left — «Siz», at pairs «Biz» — and the other on the right, by name or «Onlar»; on a watcher's phone and the screen the side of seat 0 on the left, a pair by its two first names. At «3 nəfər» and «4 nəfər» the viewer against the leader, or the runner-up when leading, and on the screen the first two, as a race's strip picks them — from `sides` in the server's order. The totals are the balances; «101» stands between the two names as the target; 👑 by the higher balance and by neither when level; the gap says «:count xal öndəsiniz», «:count xal geridəsiniz», «:name :count xal öndədir» or «Bərabərsiniz». Under a side whose account is closed: `Lock` «Hesab bağlıdır», or `Hourglass` «Gözləmədə :count» with its tries as «2/3»; an open account shows nothing there. On the screen these words stand in the gap's row; on a phone in a row of their own under it (16px, always there, so nothing moves when an account opens), the right one clear of the throw's badge — at 375px the gap's words leave them too little (`notesRow()`). While the bank holds points, «Bank: :count» stands in the gap's row. The strip stands still during a hand; at a hand's end the page saw arrive the split slides and the totals count as in a reveal, and a change of the lead between two pairs the page drew shows the ribbon. The sides stay sky and orange. For a seat's player with a player on another side the rival's half is the taunt's button, its fan with a row of names when there are two to aim at; with only computers across it is no button.
    - **The sheet** (`HandSheet`) opens by itself in the phase `sheet` on every phone — a sheet from the bottom over the table and the hand — and on the screen as a panel in the middle; nobody taps for it. Top to bottom: «Əl :number bitdi» and how — «Əli :name bitirdi», «Əl bağlandı», «Əl bərabər bağlandı», «Cavab yoxdur — daşlar yenidən paylanır» —; every seat's tiles left (`sheet.left`), face up, small, with their count, and «0-0 tək qalıb: 10 xal» where it applies; in a blocked hand each side's count and the reason in a sentence («Az xal qalan tərəf udur: 24 < 36»); then every line of `sheet.lines` as a sentence, in order, each beside its side's colour mark and an icon — «+24 rəqibin əlindən → hesaba yazıldı», «+8 → gözləməyə (13-dən az)», «Hesab açıldı: +20 və gözləmədəki 12», «Rəqibin gözləmədəki 8 xalı yandı», «Üçüncü dəfə 13 tutulmadı: gözləmədəki 11 xal yandı», «Bərabərlik: 24 xal banka getdi», «Bank alındı: +24», «Bank yandı: 24» —; the balances after it with «101-ə qədər: :count»; who opens next and why (`sheet.next`: «Növbəti əli :name açır: əli o bitirdi», «… ən az xal onda qaldı», «… əli o bağladı», «Növbəti əli ən kiçik qoşası olan açır»; nothing when `sheet.next` is null — the game is over); and a bar that runs out at `until_ms`, when the next hand is dealt. Every number is the state's but «101-ə qədər: :count», the one subtraction a page makes (`toTarget()`: the target less the balance, never below 0). Each seat's tiles left are a row of their own, its name never squeezed out by its tiles. On the screen's panel the sheet stands in two columns — the hand's tiles and counts beside the account, the balances, who opens next and the bar (`sheetColumns()`) — fitted to the table it covers (`ScreenFit`): nobody scrolls a screen on a wall. A burnt amount is said in words with `Flame`, never by a colour alone.
    - **The end.** A game that is over shows, under «Oyun bitdi»: who won — «Siz qalib gəldiniz», «:names qalib gəldi» — the score («101 : 43») and, after a dry win, «Quru qələbə» with an icon; then the final (`FinalStanding`) over the game's places — each winner's row with `Trophy` and the points the game gave it less its taunts, as every final draws them — «3 xal», «6 xal», «5 xal» with «−1» beside it where it threw one —, a computer's row with `Bot` and «0 xal» — with its confetti, hits, roll, fanfare and cheer as after any game; then the last hand's sheet, the table as it ended (on the screen a third of its height, the line alone), and the room's standing as the plain card. «Yenidən oyna» plays the same table again. A game the host stopped shows «Oyun dayandırıldı» and no final.
    - **The screen** (`ScreenDomino`): the head as for a round («101», «30 san · Para 2×2», «Əl :number»); the strip in `vh` under it; then the table at the height that is left — the line large, turning inside it, the seats on its four sides with their names large, their tile backs, the turn's ring and seconds, «PAS», `Bot`, the boneyard at one corner — and never a hand: the screen has no tiles to show. The sheet opens over the table by itself, in two columns fitted to it; a taunt's reaction covers the whole screen as in a round. Outside a game it is the start screen and, after one, the final, larger.
    - **Motion** (ADR 025's rules; ADR 038 decision 52). A hand the page saw open deals, in that moment alone — before its first act (`dealtNow()`): the viewer's tiles come in one after another; a tile that comes later is drawn as what it is, a draw at its step, or still. A play the page saw arrive lifts its tile (90 ms, its shadow — a layer of its own under it — growing), flies it from its seat's chip to its place, turning to lie as the line has it (280 ms), and settles it (140 ms), 510 ms in all («oturma», ADR 038 decision 89); its sound plays as it settles. The flight starts once the table was measured at the size it has and is gone once it lands, so a play is never drawn twice (`flies()`, `flyReady()`, `flightFrames()`). The viewer's own play does not fly: it stands at its end from the tap (rule "Taps"), and the steps after it are drawn on its phone as everywhere (ADR 038 "As built (Y7)" d2.1, d2.8). Then `last.then` a step at a time — a draw 550 ms, bringing tiles from the pile; a pass 900 ms, «PAS» popping —, and the seat to move's turn begins as the last step ends; one pure timeline gives these moments (`turnSteps()` in `domino/turn.ts`), the same that times the sounds. The sheet rises when the hand the page saw end ends. Transform and opacity only. A page opened on a position, a state more than one ply ahead and every state fetched again draw everything still, at once. Nothing moves differently for a play that wins. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing travels, turns or pops — the tile is at its end, the count is the new count, the sheet is there — and the seconds still count.
    - **Sound** ("Sound" above; ADR 038 decision 53). The room's speaker — the screen, or every phone in a room without one — plays `countdown` to `starts_ms` and `go`, then for each play the page saw arrive `tile` as it settles — a double too («eyni»: no `double` is built); `draw` at each drawing step and `knock` at each pass; `clock` in the last 5 s of the seat to move, ending at `flag_ms` and stopped by the play; `hand` as the sheet rises; at the end `close` after the last play's `tile`, and the final's cues. The seat to move's phone plays its own `turn` as its turn begins — also when a screen carries the room — under its mute; `undo` for «Geri»; `offer` with «Hazıram»'s call after the game. A seat's taps are silent. Never `sent`, `right`, `wrong`, `move`, `capture` or `crown`. `tile`, `draw`, `knock`, `hand` and `turn` are what the owner chose for the slots «qoy», «bazar», «pas», «əl sonu» and «növbə» of Y3's page (ADR 038 decision 90: four Freesound recordings, CC0-1.0, and `turn` ours — a fingertip tapping a tile twice); «qoşa» is «eyni». Files of `content/rooms/sounds.json` (version 6) made by `scripts/room-sounds/make.py`. An away seat's phone plays no `turn` and does not buzz.
    - **Words.** Every string is in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json`: `rooms.setup.families.domino`, `rooms.mechanics.domino_101.{name,short,line}`, `rooms.turn.{label,level,line}`, `rooms.table.{label,two,three,four,pairs,seats,seat,pair,computers,partner,you_play,watching}`, `rooms.domino.{hand_number,your_turn,open_with,open_any,their_turn,watching,starts_in,pick_end,left_end,right_end,undo,illegal,play_failed,yield,yielded,drew,drew_own,pass,pass_own,stood_in,played_for,away,back,boneyard,reserve,closed,partner_badge,tile,off_edge,table_label,line,line_empty,hand_label,seat_tiles,won,won_you,dry,stopped}`, `rooms.domino.sheet.{title,finished,blocked,tied,void,left,lone_blank,lower,to_target,next,next_double,until}`, `rooms.domino.sheet.line.{gained,opened,waited,released,burnt_rival,burnt_third,bank_added,bank_taken,bank_burnt}`, `rooms.domino.sheet.why.{lowest_double,lowest_tile,finished,fewest,blocker,again,given}`, `rooms.rivalry.{us,them,target,closed,waiting,bank}`; `privacy.rooms.stored` gains a domino game's plays. English says "Domino" and "101", "boneyard", "pass", "double". The game is named by what one does; no text promises a benefit to memory, mind or health, and none says a coin, a stake, a rating, a level or a rank (`RoomsWordingTest`).
    - **Nothing more.** No «Bazar» or «Pas» to tap; no hint of a good play, count of the tiles played or mark on a weak one; no undo, pause, resignation or replay; no hand on the screen and no other seat's tile on any device before a hand ends; no avatar, gift, emoji but a taunt's, chat or ready-made message; no sound or voice for a count or a result; no colour of a person; nothing typed; and no picture, sound or text of the app studied.
