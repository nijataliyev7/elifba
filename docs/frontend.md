# Elifba frontend — UI rules

How Elifba screens look and behave. The rules are adapted from the owner's precedent (the owner's earlier Laravel project) to an app with three kinds of screen: the teacher's pages (laptop and phone), the **board** on the classroom wall, and the **remote** on the teacher's phone. Change this file first when a rule changes; a review checks new UI against it.

Step A2 shipped the starter kit's screens (welcome, authentication, settings), all translated. The kit's empty dashboard is gone: a signed-in teacher lands on Siniflər. The rules below apply to every screen added from A3 on.

## Building blocks

| Layer      | Where                                                                              | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tokens     | `resources/css/app.css`                                                            | Colours, radius, sidebar and chart tokens for light and `.dark`; `font-sans` (Inter) for the interface and `font-board` (Andika) for text children read, both self-hosted in `resources/fonts/`.                                                                                                                                               |
| Primitives | `resources/js/components/ui`                                                       | shadcn/ui from the starter kit. Not linted or formatted; keep them close to upstream. Their few built-in words (`Close`, `More`…) use `useTranslation()`.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Shared     | `resources/js/components`                                                          | The shell (`app-header`, `app-sidebar`, `bottom-nav`, `area-pills`, `user-menu`, `breadcrumbs`; the nav definition in `components/app/nav-areas.ts`, the roles' icons in `components/app/roles.ts`), the kit's parts (`heading`, `text-link`, `input-error`…) and Elifba's own (`language-switcher`). From A3 a kit grows in `components/app`. |
| Features   | `resources/js/components/{classes,students,children,games,rankings,share,welcome}` | Screens built from the shared parts (A3 on; `children` from R4).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Game       | `resources/js/game/`                                                               | The board's engine, contract and Phaser scenes (A6 on); React owns everything around the canvas.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Use a shared component when one fits; add to the shared layer instead of copying markup into a feature folder.

## Rules

### 1. Layouts

- `resources/js/app.tsx` picks the layout by page name: `welcome`, `privacy` and `credits` (public pages) have none, `auth/*` uses `AuthLayout`, `settings/*` uses `AppLayout` + `SettingsLayout`, everything else `AppLayout` (sidebar shell). The board (`games/board` from A6, `lovhe` from A7), the remote (`games/remote`, A7) and the phone's pairing pages (`pult/*`, A7) have no app shell: full screen, no sidebar.
- There is no dashboard. A signed-in account lands through `/start` (Fortify's `home`, architecture §1 "Accounts and roles"): a teacher on Siniflər (`/classes`), a parent on Uşaqlarım (`/children`, rule 12), an account without a role on the role choice (`/role`, `auth/choose-role`: two cards, «Müəllim» and «Valideyn», then that role's agreement). `/dashboard` is an unnamed 302 to `/start` for old bookmarks.
- `AppLayout` is the teacher shell, after the owner's precedent. It navigates between **areas** and the **pages** inside an area, never with browser-style work tabs or a "More" sheet:
    - **One nav definition**, `components/app/nav-areas.ts`, one navigation per area (R2): the teacher's Siniflər (`/classes`), Sözlər (`/words`) and Ayarlar; the parent's Uşaqlarım (`/children`) and the same Ayarlar (Profil, Təhlükəsizlik, Görünüş və dil, Oyun — `/settings/play`, the phone remote on or off, architecture §4 "Pairing" — and Rollar, `/settings/roles`), each with a translation key, a lucide icon and its pages. The sidebar, the bottom bar and the pill strip all read the navigation of the page's area (`useNavAreas()`, from the shared `auth.area`: a page inside an area shows that area's, the shared and account pages the account's); an account without a role has none. The open area and page come from the URL (`lib/nav-active.ts`, covered by Vitest), never from stored state: every page under `/classes` (a class, its rankings, podium, game setup) belongs to Siniflər, and every page under `/children` (a child, a home game's setup) to Uşaqlarım. A new teacher page joins an area by its URL; a new area is one entry in the definition.
    - **Header** (sticky, `h-14`, every viewport): the sidebar toggle (`lg+` only), the brand linking home (`/start`), and on the right the user menu (`UserMenu`): name and email, the area switch (Rol: Müəllim / Valideyn, `PUT /area`; only for an account with both roles), language, theme, Ayarlar and Çıxış.
    - **`lg+` (≥1024px)**: the collapsible icon sidebar (`collapsible="icon"`, open or closed in the `sidebar_state` cookie, `SidebarRail` on its edge). An area with one page is a link; an area with more is a collapsible group, open while one of its pages is, and collapsed to an icon it lists its pages in a flyout menu. The sidebar holds navigation only.
    - **Below `lg`**: no sidebar (`useIsMobile` breaks at 1024 and the toggle is hidden, so the drawer cannot open). A fixed bottom tab bar (`BottomNav`) switches areas and marks the open one; it pads for the safe area, and the content is padded so nothing hides under it. An area with two or more pages shows them as a sticky pill strip under the header (`AreaPills`; today only Ayarlar). `SettingsLayout` has no nav list of its own.
    - **Breadcrumbs** show under the header on every viewport when a page has two or more, so a deep class page always has a way back (Siniflər › 3A › Reytinq). A single crumb repeats the page title and is not shown.
- **Auth pages.** Every `auth/*` page shares `layouts/auth/auth-simple-layout.tsx`. On `lg+` a quiet brand side (5/12 of the width, `green-50`, dark `green-950/30`) carries the logo linking home, a few of the logo's balloons as faint decoration and the welcome page's promise (`welcome.hero.title`, `welcome.hero.who`); the form column has the language switch at the top right, the page's title and description centred over a `max-w-sm` column, and the footer (Məxfilik, Müəlliflər). On a phone it is one column: the logo and the compact language switch, the page, the footer.
    - Login and registration put Google first when the instance offers it (the `googleSignIn` prop, ADR 008): the full-width «Google ilə daxil ol» / «Google ilə qeydiyyatdan keç» button (`GoogleSignIn`), a quiet «və ya e-poçt ilə» divider (`AuthDivider`), then the e-mail form, and the link to the other page last. Without Google the form stands alone, with no divider, and the first field takes the focus. A server `status` shows above everything in `AuthStatus`.
    - The Google button follows Google's sign-in branding guidelines and nothing comes from Google: the light theme (white, a 1px `#747775` border, `#1F1F1F` text) and in dark mode the dark one (`#131314`, `#8E918F`, `#E3E3E3`) with the unchanged «G» on its white circle, 12px padding, Google's approved wording translated (`auth.google.sign_in`, `sign_up`, `confirm`). Its text is Inter, not Google Sans: no font loads from Google, and Google Sans is not in `resources/fonts/`.
    - Tab order is the DOM order; no explicit `tabIndex`. «Şifrəni unutmusunuz?» sits on the «Məni xatırla» row, after the password.
- A page sets its layout props statically: `Login.layout = { title: 'auth.login.title', description: 'auth.login.description' }` or `Profile.layout = { breadcrumbs: [{ title: 'settings.profile.title', href: edit() }] }`. Those values are **translation keys**; the layout translates them. A page that changes them at run time calls `setLayoutProps` with keys too.
- Nav items (`NavItem.title`) and breadcrumbs (`BreadcrumbItem.title`) are translation keys as well, translated where they are rendered.

### 2. Language

- Every interface string is in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json` (Azerbaijani is the base) and is read with `const { t } = useTranslation()`. The parity test fails on a missing key, an extra key, an empty value or different `:placeholders`.
- Keys are dotted and grouped by screen: `auth.login.*`, `settings.profile.*`, `nav.*`, `common.*` for words used everywhere (`common.save`, `common.cancel`, `common.password`…). Reuse a `common.*` key before adding a new one.
- Placeholders use `:name`; `t('words.edit.button', { word })`. Plurals use `{one}…|{other}…` with a numeric `count`; Azerbaijani keeps the noun singular after a number, so its two forms are usually equal.
- Never join translated fragments into a sentence: Azerbaijani word order differs from English. Give the whole sentence one key with placeholders.
- The language switch sits in the auth pages' footer, the welcome page's header and Settings → Appearance and language (`LanguageSwitcher`), and in the teacher shell's user menu in the header (`UserMenu`), on every viewport. Both call `PUT /locale`; the server keeps the choice.
- Child-facing game text (prompts, words, sentences) is **content**, always Azerbaijani, and never goes into `lang/*.json`.
- Write in plain words a teacher uses. Code comments are English.

### 3. Page header and actions

- Each page starts with one `h1` or a `Heading` and, beside it, the page's main action. A short intro is one line under the title.
- Each teacher page declares its breadcrumbs through its layout props.

### 4. Buttons

- The main action of a form or page is the default `<Button>`; secondary controls are `outline` or `ghost`; deleting is `destructive` and asks first in a `Dialog`.
- A navigating action is an Inertia `<Link>` (with `Button asChild` when it should look like a button), never a button with `router.visit`. The one exception leaves the app: the Google buttons (`GoogleSignIn`) are plain `<a>` links to our own `/auth/google/*` routes, whose answer is a redirect to Google (architecture §7 "Google sign-in").
- A form's submit button shows `Spinner` while processing and is disabled meanwhile.
- On the board and the remote, targets are big (at least 64px on the remote's item buttons) and never depend on hover.
- A prompt the board says (architecture §3 "Voice") has a round 🔊 button (`Volume2`) after its text, framed in the pack's accent like the target, 64px on the projector (80px from `xl`, 44px below `lg`), labelled «Tapşırığı yenidən səsləndir»; it says the prompt again. There is none when the round has no clip, the account turned the prompt's voice off or the board is silent. The voice itself belongs to the canvas (`voice.ts`, `BoardScene`), never to React.
- The board's silent mode (architecture §4 "Board in the browser") is a toggle button labelled «Səssiz» with `aria-pressed`: outline while the board plays sound, filled while it is silent. Its icon is a crossed-out speaker (`VolumeOff`) in both states, never `Volume2`, so it cannot be taken for the 🔊 that says the prompt again. It sits in the header and, before «Başla», on the start screen beside «Pultu qoş».
- «Kim oynayır?» (architecture §4 "Board without a phone") is a dialog with the shared `StudentGrid`, opened by «Başla» or «Davam et» in a class game with students: a tap on a choice starts the game, so it never has a confirm button.
- The board's voice (architecture §3 "Voice") belongs to the canvas, never to React: the page hands the stage who may be at the board as ids with their `name_audio` paths (`VoicePlayers`) and a fetcher with the board's own `auth` (`nameClip()`), and renders nothing for a name said. No name is said when «Kim oynayır?» picks a student, and nothing is voiced on the result screen's text, the podium, the rankings, the share card or the remote.
- A student's or a child's name that the board says aloud (ADR 016, `has_voice` true) carries `NameVoiceMark` after it: a 16px `Volume2` in `text-muted-foreground` (20px beside the child page's title), labelled «Lövhə bu adı səslə deyir» for screen readers and on hover. It is information, never a button, and is the same icon as 🔊 only because both mean "the board says this". A name without a clip gets no mark of its own: the class page's student list has one muted line under it when any active student's name has none (`students.voice.missing`), and the child's page one under the name (`children.voice.missing`). A name Elifba's name list spells otherwise («Lale» for «Lalə», `voice_spelling`) has `NameSpellingFix` under it on the class page's student list and on the child's page: one muted line, «Ad lüğətində belə yazılır: «Lalə»», and an `outline` «Düzəlt» button (labelled «Adı «Lalə» kimi düzəlt»; 44px on the child's page) that saves that spelling through the row's own update route, a refusal shown as a toast; never on the child card, which is a link. With `has_voice` null (the account's names or praise off) there is no mark, line or fix. Archived students and the child cards' reorder list show none.
- Settings → Oyun's voice form has three checkboxes in order, «Tapşırığı səslə de», «Tərif səsi» and «Adı səslə de», each with its hint; the third is unticked and disabled (its label dimmed) while «Tərif səsi» is off, and keeps the account's choice in its hidden field for when praise is on again.

### 5. Forms

- Forms are Inertia forms (`<Form {...action.form()}>` with Wayfinder, or `useForm`). Validation stays on the server (`*Data::rules()`, framework messages in `lang/{az,en}/validation.php`); `InputError` shows the field's message.
- Labels are visible (`Label htmlFor`); placeholders only show an example.
- Never show ids or codes as something to type, except the 6-digit pairing code (A7), which is made for that.

### 6. Spacing, grids and phones

- Every grid starts with `grid-cols-1` and adds columns from a breakpoint: `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2`.
- Nothing may widen a 375px screen: give flex children `min-w-0`, truncate long names, let wide tables scroll inside their own container.
- The teacher shell's breakpoint is `lg` (1024px): the sidebar from `lg`, the bottom tab bar and the pill strip below it. A phone and a tablet get the same navigation.
- The remote is used one-handed on a phone: its controls sit in the lower half of the screen.
- The board also opens on a phone or tablet, but it is made for the projector: from `lg` up its layout is the projector's and stays as it is; below `lg` the same parts shrink around the canvas, which keeps its 16:9 (Phaser's `Scale.FIT`; there is no portrait game layout). Below `lg` the header's controls are 44px icon buttons whose label stays for screen readers (`max-lg:sr-only`), «Keç», «Fasilə/Davam», «Səssiz» and «Bitir» among them, one tap away; the curtains and the result use smaller type; the start screen puts its title and «Başla» beside the pairing card in landscape; the pairing card stacks below `sm` with a QR of at least 144px, and during play it opens in a dialog instead of the corner of the canvas. In portrait a short hint asks to turn the phone sideways, and «Başla» tries to lock landscape after entering full screen. «Tam ekran» shows only where `document.fullscreenEnabled` is true (never on an iPhone). Leaving the board leaves full screen and unlocks the orientation, so the next page stands upright again; a guest board keeps both between its games. The «Kim oynayır?» dialog scrolls inside itself on a phone held sideways. The board page never scrolls; a curtain too tall for the screen scrolls inside itself.

### 7. Colour, type and theme

- Use the tokens (`bg-background`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-primary`, `text-destructive`…), not raw palette classes, on teacher pages. The board's look comes from the theme pack's palette (architecture §2), not from the app's tokens. A pack's backdrop (architecture §8 "Backdrops") fills the whole board page behind the header, the canvas and the footer, so its sky and ground reach the screen's edges around the 16:9 canvas. It is pale and never lowers the contrast of a tile or a letter: tiles, plates, the prompt and the footer's cards keep their own opaque faces, and text drawn straight on the page (the round count, the prompt's question) sits on its sky.
- The medal colours of places 1–3 (gold, silver, bronze; `components/share/place-colors.ts`, from OpenMoji's palette) are the one palette exception on teacher pages: the podium page and the share card use them alike, with dark text in both themes. The share card has one fixed light look and never follows dark mode.
- **The logo** (architecture §8 "Elifba's mark") is a balloon from the `find` game with «ə» cut out, drawn as SVG paths (`AppLogoIcon`, one colour from the caller's `fill-*` class). Its green, `#15803D` (`bg-green-700`), is the other palette exception: the logo's tile (white mark on green) in the header, the sidebar, the auth pages, the welcome page and the install banner (rule 11), the auth pages' brand side (`green-50`) and the welcome page's main action. The master files are `resources/images/brand/mark.svg` (one colour) and `tile.svg` (on the green tile); `public/favicon.svg` is `tile.svg`, and `public/images/og.png` shows the tile. The app icons are drawn from `tile.svg` by `php scripts/app-icons/generate.php` (architecture §10 "Installing Elifba").
- Every teacher screen works in light and dark; check both. The board is always light: its page holds `forceLightTheme()` (`use-appearance`) while it is mounted, so the dialogs it opens are light too.
- Text children read — the board's prompts, items and words, the remote's item buttons — uses `font-board` (Andika, made for beginning readers: clear a, g, I/l and Ə ə Ğ ğ İ ı). Everything else uses `font-sans` (Inter).

### 8. Inertia, not a SPA router

- Links are Inertia `<Link>` (except the Google buttons, rule 4); visits are `router.get/put/...` with `preserveScroll` where the page stays.
- Shared state comes from Inertia props (`usePage().props`: `auth`, `locale`, `locales`, `translations`, `sidebarOpen`), not a client store. No i18next, axios, zustand, TanStack Query or react-hook-form.
- Routes are Wayfinder functions (`@/routes/*`, `@/actions/*`), never hand-written URLs.

### 9. Nothing leaves the host

- No CDN, external font, analytics, tracker or error service. Fonts and pictures are files in the repository; the font loader is `local()` in `vite.config.ts`.
- Pictures and sounds arrive as URLs in the page props (an avatar is `{ key, url }`); TypeScript never builds an asset path. A student's avatar is drawn by `StudentAvatar`.
- Children's names appear only on the teacher's pages, the board and the share card's allowed fields (architecture §6, §7), never in a URL, a log or a broadcast.

### 10. The welcome page

- `/` (`WelcomeController`, page `welcome`) is the landing page for a teacher who opens a shared link, often on a phone. In this order it says what Elifba is, how a lesson goes, what a teacher gets, what a parent does at home (R7), what children's data it keeps and what it costs (nothing), then shows the way in. A guest and a signed-in account see the same page; only the actions differ («Qeydiyyat» and «Daxil ol», or the account's own page through `/start`: «Siniflər» in the teacher area, «Uşaqlarım» in the parent area, «Rolunuzu seçin» before the role choice).
- Its facts come from the server as props and are never retyped in `lang/` or TSX: the mechanics (`Mechanic` cases, named by `games.mechanics.*`), the packs (title in the interface language and palette, `ContentRepository`), the packs a child who cannot read yet plays (`picturePacks`: the keys of the packs that list a unit `Unit::reads()` is false for), the alphabet (`Alphabet`), a few avatars (`Avatars`) and the repository's address (`elifba.source_url`). It has no testimonials, school logos, user or star counts and no ratings.
- **The wall** at the top plays real rounds: `Play\Support\SampleRounds` builds a few `find` rounds at the first letter level with the real builder, with every letter learned and one built-in pack. The pack and the letters change with the Baku date. A visitor taps an item on the wall or the same item on the phone beside it (below it on a phone), the two ways a class plays. The wall is always light, like the board, with its colours from `game/palette.ts`, and Phaser is not loaded. It plays one round by itself after the page loads, which is the page's only motion that nobody triggered. With `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not, and a hit changes the item without animation.
- Type and colour follow rule 7 with one addition. Inter is for the teacher. Andika is only for what a child reads on the wall and the phone and for letters shown as letters (the alphabet). Colour comes from the tokens, the logo's green (`#15803D`) for the main action and the step numbers, and the packs' palettes.
- **«Evdə, uşağınızla»** (R7) follows «Nə əldə edirsiniz»: the teacher's story stays first and whole, and the parent's is an addition, not a second page. A plain link under the hero's actions («Valideynsiniz? Evdə uşağınızla da oynayın») jumps to it. It has the heading and one sentence on the left; a home game on the right (the board of a picture round with the parent's remote over its corner, as in step 3); three points, not numbered and marked by a green rule (choose «Valideyn», add the child, play together; the adult reads the prompt aloud); the picture games with the `picturePacks` as pills; the reading games at home; and that a child collects stars and is never compared (ADR 011: no ranking, place, podium or winners at home).
- «Dərs necə keçir» is a sequence and the only numbered part. Its pictures are screenshots of the app with demo data and made-up names, kept in `resources/images/welcome/` as WebP. They are imported through Vite and get `width`, `height` and an `alt` in both languages, plus `loading="lazy"` below the fold.
- The GitHub button is a plain link to `elifba.source_url` with GitHub's mark as an inline SVG (`components/github-link.tsx`, architecture §8 "Brand marks"). There is no GitHub script, badge, iframe or star count.
- `resources/views/app.blade.php` prints the description, Open Graph and Twitter tags on every page from `welcome.meta.*` (via `__()`, in the page's locale). A link shared on WhatsApp then shows a title, one sentence and `public/images/og.png` (1200×630, an absolute URL on the app's own host).

### 11. The install banner

- Chrome offers to install Elifba as an app when the manifest qualifies (architecture §10 "Installing Elifba"). An inline script in `app.blade.php` catches `beforeinstallprompt` before React mounts, holds back Chrome's own install bar on every page (the board and the remote included) and keeps the event on `window.elifbaInstall`; `appinstalled` clears it. Elifba's own banner, `InstallBanner` (`components/install-banner.tsx`), makes the offer.
- **Look.** A card: the logo's tile, «Elifba-nı quraşdırın», one line on what it gives (its own window, one tap from the home screen or the desktop), «Quraşdır» (the default `Button`; it calls the event's `prompt()`, which opens Chrome's own install dialog) and «İndi yox» (`ghost`). Tokens only (`bg-popover`, `text-muted-foreground`), so it follows dark mode; it slides in only without `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Place.** It floats (`fixed`, `z-40`: over the page and the bottom tab bar, under dialogs), so nothing moves when it comes or goes: across the bottom of a phone with the 16px gutter, bottom right from `sm` (384px wide), clear of the safe areas, and in the teacher shell below `lg` above the bottom tab bar.
- **Access.** A `section` named by its heading and described by its line, so screen readers list it as a region; it is reached by Tab after the page's content and never takes the focus. Esc inside it equals «İndi yox».
- **Where.** The welcome page, the auth pages (`AuthSimpleLayout`) and the shell of either area (`AppSidebarLayout`) each mount it once; `bannerAllowedOn()` in `lib/install-banner.ts` lists the pages that may show it, and Vitest checks the list against every page in `pages/`. Never the board (`games/board`, `lovhe`), the remote (`games/remote`, `pult/*`), the podium (made for the projector), `privacy`, `credits` or the share card. A new page shows nothing until it is listed.
- **When.** Chrome has an unused offer, the app was not installed during the visit, the page does not run installed (`display-mode: standalone`, `window-controls-overlay` or `minimal-ui`) and «İndi yox» was not chosen in this browser in the last 14 days. The time of «İndi yox» is the one `localStorage` key `elifba.install-banner.dismissed-at`, read and written inside `try/catch` (without storage it holds until the page is reloaded). Cancelling Chrome's dialog counts as «İndi yox». An offer is used once; Chrome makes a new one on a later visit. Safari and Firefox never make one, so they show nothing (an iPhone adds Elifba from Share → Add to Home Screen, with `apple-touch-icon.png`).
- These rules live once, in the pure `showInstallBanner()` (`lib/install-banner.ts`, covered by Vitest); `hooks/use-install-offer.ts` only feeds it the browser's state and runs the two buttons.

### 12. The parent shell

The parent area (R4, architecture §1 "Accounts and roles", §4 "At home", §5 "A child's stars", ADR 011) is the same `AppLayout` with the parent's navigation (Uşaqlarım and Ayarlar), made for a parent holding a phone with a child of three to six beside them: few steps, big targets, nothing to compare.

- **Few steps.** From «Uşaqlarım» a game is two taps away: «Oyna» on the child's card, then «Başlat» on «Hazır oyun» at the top of the catalog, which plays the first game that can start with the child's remembered choices (rule 13); any other game is one tap more, its card. A game page's start bar sticks to the bottom of the screen, so a phone never scrolls to it.
- **Big targets.** Every control a parent taps is at least 44px (`h-11`, `size-11`); «Oyna» is 48px on a card and 56px on the child's page; an avatar to choose is a whole grid cell. Nothing depends on hover.
- **«Uşaqlarım»** (`children/index`): one card per child (`ChildCard`: the avatar, the name with `NameVoiceMark` when the board says it (rule 4), today's stars only, a link to the child's page and «Oyna»), one column on a phone and two or three from `sm`/`lg`, in the parent's own order. «Uşaq əlavə et» opens `AddChildDialog`: the first name (`ChildNameField`) and the avatar (`AvatarPicker`), with the first avatar none of the children has already chosen; the server opens the new child's page. «Sıranı dəyiş» (two children or more) turns the cards into a list with up and down (`ChildOrder`), saved at each move. At the limit (`limits.max`) the page says so instead of offering the dialog.
- **The child's page** (`children/show`): a card with the avatar, the name (with `NameVoiceMark`, or the line that the name list does not have it; rule 4), the edit button (`EditChildDialog`: name and avatar) and «Oyna»; then the child's stars (`ChildStars`: today, this week and in all as three tiles, the last seven days as rows with the server's date label, a bar and the number, and the retention note); the learned letters (`LearnedLetters`, shared with the class page); and «Uşağı sil», which asks first in a dialog. A child is deleted, never archived.
- **The setup** of a home game is rule 13's catalog with `home`: its title names the child («Yeni oyun: :name»), the picture and number shelves come before the reading ones, and a new child starts in «Asan». The phone's guest board pages pass `home` for a child too.
- **The board and the phone at home** (§4 "At home"): `BoardChild` (the avatar, «Oynayır», the name) stands where the class's student button and «Növbəti» would, on the board's footer and on the remote, and is not a button. The start screen shows the child's avatar; the pairing card does not open by itself (`pairingOnLoad()` in `lib/game-players.ts`), and «Pultu qoş» on the start screen and in the header brings it. The result screen heads with the child's avatar (beside the total below `lg`) and adds «:name artıq cəmi :count ulduz toplayıb!» — the stars before this game (`child.stars`) plus this game's.
- **Nothing to compare.** No ranking, place, podium, «best», total beside a sibling's or list ordered by stars appears anywhere in the parent area; `ChildPagesTest` checks the props of every parent page for it. Stars are amber (`fill-amber-400 text-amber-500`) on the parent's pages and the pack's accent on the board.

### 13. The game setup

«Yeni oyun» is a catalog of ready games, not a form of questions. The owner chose it on 2026-11-13 from three clickable prototypes (variant B, shelves like a game store), added «Asan / Normal» and remembered choices, and asked that a card look like its game. It replaces the three numbered questions chosen on 2026-11-11, which felt like an exam. «Son oyunlar» stays out (the owner took it out on 2026-11-11 because it muddled the page). `GameCatalog` (`components/games/game-catalog.tsx`, replacing `game-setup-form.tsx`, `setup-tiles.tsx` and `setup-start.tsx`) is the one game setup, for a class and for a child (`home`), on the setup page (`games/setup`: `/classes/{class}/games/create`, `/children/{child}/games/create`) and on the phone's guest board pages (`pult/guest-board`). It orders and explains the server's catalog (`GameSetupOptions`, architecture §2 "Game setup"): what can start is always the server's answer (`options[].startable`, the same check as `GameSetupData::rules()`), never a rule of the catalog's own. The choosing logic lives in `lib/setup-choices.ts` as pure functions with Vitest tests: which cards a mode shows, the shelf order, a card's levels and its default level, the remembered choices that still apply and the reason shown. The components only render it: `game-card.tsx` (the card), `game-scene.tsx` (its picture), `game-sheet.tsx` (a card's game page) and `setup-extras.tsx` («Əlavə»).

- **A card is a game.** One card per theme and game: a pack (`packs[]`) with a (unit, mechanic) the content offers it for. Its name comes from the server (`cards[].title`, architecture §2: «Qatarla cümlə qur», «Meyvələrlə say», «Heyvanlarla şəkil tap»); TypeScript never composes or suffixes it. A card covers every level of its unit that lists its mechanic; the level is chosen on the card's page.
- **«Asan» and «Normal».** Under the heading, a mode switch in a `bg-muted` track: two 58px buttons with `aria-pressed`, each a 38px icon square (`MousePointerClick`, `Puzzle`; `bg-primary` when chosen), the name and a muted line: «Asan» / «Yalnız vur» and «Normal» / «Tap, qur, say» (`games.setup.modes.*`, `.mode_hints.*`). «Asan» shows only the `pop` games: the child taps anything and nothing is wrong. «Normal» shows the rest: `find`, the three build games and `count`, where the board asks and the child answers. Under the switch, muted: «Rejim və seçimlər :name üçün yadda qalır.» (`games.setup.remembered`; `:name` the class's name or the child's first name). A tap switches at once and is saved at once (below). A new class starts in «Normal», a new child in «Asan».
- **The shelves.** One `section` per unit that has a card in the mode, in group order: the reading units (letter, syllable, word, sentence) first for a class, the picture and number units first at home; within a group, the content's order. «Cümlələr» has no shelf in «Asan» (a sentence has no `pop`). A shelf heads with a 40px `bg-muted` icon square (letter `CaseSensitive`, syllable `Blocks`, word `WholeWord`, sentence `Text`, picture `Image`, number `Hash`), the unit's name (`games.setup.units.*`) as an `h2` and a muted line «:hint · :count oyun» (`games.setup.shelf`, with `unit_hints.*`). Its cards are one row that scrolls sideways with `scroll-snap`, bleeding to the page's edges: 152px cards below 700px of content width, 186px from there. Within a shelf the cards follow the game order (the level's mechanics, `pop` first) and then the packs' order, with every card that can start before every locked one. Under the first reading shelf's heading stands the learned-letters line: «Oxu oyunlarında yalnız öyrənilən :count hərf işlənir:» and the letters in `font-board`, spaced, on a line of their own, or «Oxu oyunlarında bütün hərflər işlənir.» when `allLetters`, then «Dəyiş», an Inertia `Link` to `#learned-letters` on the class page or the child's page (on the guest board, no link).
- **Skill chips.** Above the shelves, one chip per shelf (its icon and name, 40px, a row that scrolls sideways) jumps to that shelf and moves the focus to its heading. Chips do not filter, and nothing is pressed.
- **«Hazır oyun».** Above the shelves, one large card: the first card that can start, in page order, with its remembered choices. It shows its scene at 4:3, a pill «Hazır oyun» (`games.setup.ready`), the card's name (22px, 26px from `lg`, bold), a muted summary line (below), «Başlat» (the default `Button`, 52px, full width below `lg`, at most 260px from `lg`; «Lövhədə başla» on the guest board) and a link «Seçimlər» (`games.setup.options`) that opens its game page, as a tap on its scene does. From `lg` the scene is a 340px column beside the text. Its background is `palette[0]` at 12% over `bg-background`. So «Oyna» → «Başlat» is still two taps (rule 12), and any other game three: «Oyna», the card, «Başlat». When nothing can start at all, `games.setup.none` and the learned-letters link replace «Hazır oyun» and the shelves.
- **The card** (`GameCard`): a `button` whose accessible name is the card's name: the scene (1:1, `rounded-[22px]`), then the name (14.5px, semibold, balanced) and one muted line, «:count səviyyə» (`games.setup.level_count`) when more than one of its levels can start, else that level's `hint`. No border and no box around the text, as in a store. With `hover:hover` the scene lifts by 2px; with `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves.
- **The scene** (`GameScene`, `aria-hidden`) shows the game before it is played. It is drawn like the board, always light whatever the theme, from the pack's palette and its cover only (no new art, ADR 014): a sky from `palette[0]` mixed with white, a sun circle in `palette[2]` at the top right, a hill in `palette[1]` along the bottom (a palette with fewer colours repeats its first), and the cover with a soft drop shadow. Over it, the card's `sample` (architecture §2) as the board would show it, in `font-board` on white with a shadow in `palette[0]`: for `pop` and `find`, the cover at the lower left and three round bubbles beside it; `find` rings the middle one and puts a `Search` badge on it, `pop` puts a `MousePointerClick` badge on the first. For a build game, the cover above a row of tiles: the first tile filled, the rest dashed empty slots. For `count`, three small copies of the cover above three digit bubbles, the middle ringed. At a picture level, the cover alone and large, ringed for `find`. Badges and rings are `palette[0]` mixed with the board's ink, never black. Sizes are container query units (`cqw`), so the same scene draws a 152px card, «Hazır oyun» and the game page.
- **The game page** (`GameSheet`). A tap on a card opens it: below `lg` a full-screen `Dialog` over the catalog, from `lg` a 390px `Sheet` on the right; the catalog stays where it was. From top to bottom: the scene at 4:3, full width, with a round 42px button over its corner («Oyunlara qayıt» with `ChevronLeft` below `lg`, «Bağla» with `X` from `lg`; Esc closes); two pills (the unit's icon and name; the mode's icon and «Asan» or «Normal»); the card's name as the dialog's title (24px, bold); the mechanic's line (`games.setup.mechanic_hints.*`, `pop_say` at a level that does not read); **«Səviyyə»** when two of the card's levels exist: two option cards in a row (`aria-pressed`, the level's title over its `hint`, the chosen one with a `border-primary` ring on `bg-accent`); with one level, its hint as a muted line; **«Əlavə»** (below); the muted line ««Başlat»dan sonra bu seçimlər :name üçün yadda qalır.» (`games.setup.remembered_after_start`); and the start bar, sticky at the bottom with the safe-area padding: the summary line, centred, over «Başlat» (52px, full width; «Lövhədə başla» on the guest board) with `Spinner` while processing. The summary is the level's title followed, where they apply, by «böyük hərflər» or «kiçik hərflər», «:count hərf» and «yalnız mənim sözlərim», joined with « · » (`games.setup.summary.*`).
- **«Əlavə».** As before: a block closed by default, a 52px button (`aria-expanded`) with «Əlavə» and, muted, its values («böyük hərflər · bütün hərflər»); it opens by itself when the server returns an error on one of its fields; a game whose unit does not read has none. Inside, in this order: «Hərflərin yazılışı» (reading units), two buttons, each the first three learned letters in `font-board` over «Böyük hərflər» / «Kiçik hərflər»; «Hansı hərflər soruşulsun?» (the letter level only), «Hamısı (:count)» first, then one 44px toggle per learned letter, with «Az hərf seçsəniz, yanlarına oxşar hərflər əlavə olunur.»; «Hansı sözlər?» (only where `own` is not null): when `own.startable`, «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» and «Hamısı»; otherwise, in the teacher area only, `no_own_words` is one muted line with a link to «Sözlər» and any other reason is that reason with `AddWordDialog`; the parent area shows nothing then.
- **Remembered choices** (the owner, 2026-11-13: a choice made once is kept). Each class and each child has its own (architecture §2 "Remembered setup"): the mode, the level per unit, the letter case per unit, the chosen letters and «Yalnız mənim sözlərim». The mode is saved when it is tapped; the choices on a game page are saved when that game starts, so what is kept is what was played. A game page opens with the remembered level of its unit when that level can start with this card, else the first that can; the remembered letter case of its unit, else the server's default (capitals at the letter level, as written otherwise); the remembered letters still learned (none when fewer than two are left); the remembered «Yalnız mənim sözlərim» where it can start, else on where it can start. Closing a game page without starting forgets what was changed on it. The guest board uses the chosen players' choices and saves them the same way.
- **Locked.** A game the content does not offer for a pack has no card. A card whose options exist but none can start (`startable` false: too few learned letters, words the owner hid leave too few, or the board cannot draw it yet) is shown locked, never hidden: its scene in greyscale at 45% opacity, a 30px white `Lock` badge over its top right corner, its name in `text-muted-foreground` and its line «Daha çox hərf lazımdır» (`games.setup.locked.letters`) for a learned-letters shortage (`too_few_letters`, `too_few_syllables`, `too_few_words`, `too_few_sentences`), else the reason, or «Tezliklə» when `drawable` is false. It is `aria-disabled` but focusable, and a tap opens its game page with «Bu oyun hələ açılmır.» (`games.setup.locked.game`), the full reason of its first level and «Hərfləri dəyiş» (the setup page only), and «Başlat» disabled. On a game page that can start, a locked level is a dashed option card with `Lock` and «Daha çox hərf lazımdır», never chosen; a tap shows its full reason and «Hərfləri dəyiş» under the row.
- **Look.** Tokens only outside the scene, so dark mode follows (rule 7); the scene stays light like the board. Every choice is a `button type="button"`, with `aria-pressed` where it toggles; every target is at least 44px and nothing depends on hover. The game page posts `mechanic`, `pack`, `level`, `letter_case`, `letters[]` and `own_words` (no `rounds`: the server's default); server errors show with `InputError` on the game page, and an error on a hidden field (the class or the child, on the guest board) at the top.
- **The guest board** (`pult/guest-board`): «Gedən oyunlar» names each live game «:players» over «:level · :mechanic · :status» (`games.setup.summary.game`). «Yeni oyun» first asks «Kim oynayır?» (one 44px button per child, then per class, the first chosen), then shows the catalog for those players: mode switch, «Hazır oyun», shelves and game pages, with «Lövhədə başla».
- **Entry points.** On the class page «Oyun başlat» is the main action (the first header button, the only `default` one, on a full-width row of its own below `sm`). `LearnedLetters` (class page and child's page) has `id="learned-letters"` and a `scroll-mt` of the sticky header's height. «Uşaqlarım» and the child's page keep «Oyna» (rule 12).
- **The board's start screen** (not built yet) is to say the card's name: as its title `cards[].title` of the game («Qatarla cümlə qur»), above it who plays, under it «:level · :count raund», so the adult and the child see one name from the card to the wall. `BoardProps` is to carry that title (architecture §2).
- **Words.** Every string above is a key in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json` (rule 2). New: `games.names.{unit}_{mechanic}`, the fourteen games' names: «Hərf vur», «Hərf tap», «Heca vur», «Heca tap», «Heca qur», «Söz vur», «Söz tap», «Söz yığ», «Cümlə qur», «Şəkil vur», «Şəkil tap», «Rəqəm vur», «Rəqəm tap», «Say» ("Tap letters", "Find the letter", "Tap syllables", "Find the syllable", "Build a syllable", "Tap words", "Find the word", "Build the word", "Build a sentence", "Tap pictures", "Find the picture", "Tap numbers", "Find the number", "Count"); `games.titles.{unit}_{mechanic}`, the card names with the pack's `:with` («:with hərf tap», ":game :with" in English: "Find the letter :with"); `games.setup.mode`, `.modes.{easy,normal}`, `.mode_hints.{easy,normal}`, `.remembered`, `.remembered_after_start`, `.ready`, `.options`, `.level_count`, `.shelf`, `.skills` (the chips' group label «Bacarıqlar»), `.back` («Oyunlara qayıt») and `.locked.game`. Kept: `games.setup.title`, `.child_title`, `.level`, `.units.*`, `.unit_hints.*`, `.level_hints.*`, `.mechanic_hints.*`, `.learned.*`, `.extra` and its fields, `.summary.*`, `.locked.{letters,change}`, `.none`, `.start`, `.coming_soon`, `.errors.*`, `pult.guest_board.*`, and `games.mechanics.*` for the board, the remote and the welcome page. Gone: `games.setup.unit`, `.mechanic`, `.pack`, `.groups.{reading,pictures}`, `.starting`, `.replaced.{mechanic,pack}` and `.locked.unit`: there are no questions to ask, and a card is one choice, so nothing else is replaced.

### 14. Choosing the rounds, playing again and leaving the board

The owner's choices of 2026-11-13, on every board (the teacher board, the home board and the guest board alike, unless said otherwise); the server's side is architecture §2 "Game setup" («Yenidən oyna») and §4 "Round count".

- **The round count under «Başla».** While the start screen waits for «Başla» and nothing is answered, a small group under the button, «Raund sayı» (`games.board.round_count`) over one button per step — every multiple of 5 from `roundLimits.min` to `.max`, plus the game's own count when it is none of them (`lib/round-steps.ts`) — 48px on the projector, 44px below `lg`, tabular figures, with `aria-pressed`. The game's count is filled in the pack's accent; the others are `outline`. A tap rebuilds the rounds on the server: every button and «Başla» are disabled until it answers, and the one asked for shows `Spinner`; then the line above, «:label · :count raund», shows the new count. A failure is the board's usual notice (`games.board.notices.rounds_failed`). Once anything is answered — «Davam et» — the group is gone, never shown disabled. Never a typed number.
- **«Oyunu bitir» looks like leaving.** In the header it is the last control, after a thin divider that sets it apart from «Keç» and «Fasilə»: an `outline` button tinted red (`border-red-300 text-red-700`, the board's own palette, rule 7) with `LogOut`, a 44px icon below `lg` like the others. Its label and its confirm dialog («Oyun bitsin?», the `destructive` «Bitir») stay.
- **The result offers what comes next, most likely first.** «Yenidən oyna» (`games.board.replay`) is the main action: a solid button in the pack's accent with `RotateCcw`, a form posting to `/games/{game}/replay`, with `Spinner` while it starts the game; the new game's board opens in its place (full screen is kept, rule 6). «Yeni oyun» follows as an `outline` link with `Plus`. Leaving — «Sinfə qayıt» or «Uşağın səhifəsinə qayıt» — stands apart below a thin rule as a `ghost` link with `LogOut`. All three wait, disabled, while answers are still being saved. A guest board shows none of them: its result waits for the phone's next game, with its own «Mənim deyil» and «Lövhəni bağla».
