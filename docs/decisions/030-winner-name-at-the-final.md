- **Why it is hard to reverse:** once a room has heard its winner named and cheered, the final without it sounds unfinished; and the route that gives a name clip to a room's screen is a new way a name can travel, which must stay ids only from the first day — a URL or a log line with a hash, once written, carries the name.

## Context

ADR 028 made the room's sound set; its final opens the places from the last to the first, with a hit for the third and the second, a roll ending where the first place opens (`finalTimeline().winnerAt`) and a fanfare from there. The owner asked on 2026-11-18 that the big screen then say the winner's first name, and decided: only at the final, only for the winner, only on the screen; only the clip that already exists in the name list — no new phrase, no call to a speech provider, no new clip; a name off the list stays silent. On hearing the first build in a live game (2026-11-19) the owner asked that «Əla oynadın!» follow the name, as a home game ends on the parent's board: a phrase that already exists as a clip, so nothing new is made.

The clips are ADR 016's: 924 first names in `content/names.json`, one clip each in `content/voice-names/{hash}.mp3` — the name alone, in the voice's excited take («Nihat!»), made once by `content:voice` and committed. `Content\Support\NameVoices` decides what can be said: `file($displayName)` gives the clip of a display name's first name (`Language\Support\PersonName::firstName()`), or null. A clip's hash can be computed from the public list and settings, so a clip has no public URL: a board gets one by ids through its game. A home board ends a game with a solved round by the child's name, a 150 ms pause and «Əla oynadın!»; a class board, when several students played, with «Əla oynadınız!» and no name; a name off the list leaves the phrase alone (ADR 016 decisions 4 and 7). The two phrases are public built-in clips (`voice.json`'s `finish` and `finish_class`, `VoiceClips::url()`).

A room's player is an account shown under `users.name`, whatever the account wrote; a device knows a player only by `room_players.id`. The list was drawn up for children born from about 2015, so many adults' first names are not on it.

## Decision

1. **Only at the final, only for the first place, only on the screen.** When the screen's page hears the final begin (`game.phase = over`, ADR 025 decision 8) and someone holds the first place, it cheers the first place after the fanfare (the cue `cheer`). The first place is the final's — the room's standing after the game, the place the confetti and the fanfare celebrate. Never at a reveal, in the lobby, for the second or the third place, on a phone or on any page but `rooms/screen`; nobody scored, no fanfare and no cheer.

2. **The cheer is the board's end, with the clips that exist.** The first place's first name — the name list's clip, «Nihat!» — then, `PHRASE_GAP_MS` (150 ms, the board's pause) later, «Əla oynadın!»: «Nihat! … Əla oynadın!», as a home game ends. The phrase is the board's own clip (`finish`); no new phrase, no new clip, no speech provider, no speech synthesis. Whether an account's name can be said is `NameVoices::file(users.name)`, unchanged — «Nihat Əliyev» and «Əliyev Nihat» are said «Nihat», «Lale» is said «Lalə», «Ayşən-Nur», «nicat_77» or a name off the list is silent — and the room adds no matching of its own. A name that cannot be said leaves the phrase alone, as on the board. The screen's props carry the two phrases' public URLs as `finish` (`{one, many}`, `Rooms\Support\RoomFinishVoice`); a phone's do not.

3. **After the fanfare.** The cheer is a cue of the final moment, placed at `winnerAt + FANFARE_MS`: the fanfare's length, 2.30 s (`scripts/room-sounds/README.md`), so the name follows its last chord. It is played at the screen's level times `VOICE_GAIN`, which sets it with the effects (the name and phrase clips are levelled to −16 LUFS, the room's set to −20 LUFS: −3.6 dB).

4. **A shared first place.** Each holder's name, in the state's ranking order (the server's, never re-sorted), one after another with `NAME_GAP_MS` (300 ms) between them — at most three (`MAX_NAMES`) — then «Əla oynadınız!» (`finish_class`), as a class board ends a game several played. When four or more share the first place, no name is said and «Əla oynadınız!» is said alone. A holder whose name has no clip is skipped and the others are said.

5. **The route: ids only, for whoever may open the screen.** `GET /rooms/{room}/members/{member}/voice` (`rooms.members.voice`, `RoomMemberVoiceController`, `Rooms\Support\RoomMemberVoice`) in the room group: `{room}` and `{member}` are ULIDs, `{member}` bound only inside its room (`scopeBindings()`), and the route passes `can:screen` — the host of an open room, in the host's own session, who may open the screen. A row the host removed, a row whose account is gone, and a name without a clip are a 404 before a file is read; a player of the room (a phone) and a closed room are refused by the policy; an account outside the room gets the binding's 404; with the switch off it is a 404 like every room route. The answer is the children's: `audio/mpeg`, `Cache-Control: no-store, private` (no shared cache keeps it, and the screen's browser keeps no copy on disk), `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and no header naming the file; a file that cannot be read is a 404, never a warning that would carry its path. The access log reads `/rooms/01K…/members/01K…/voice`. The route is not limited to the final's first place: the same session's state already lists every member's name, a clip says nothing more, and when a name is said is the page's rule, as on the board. The route uses `NameVoices` (`Content`); nothing of `Play` or of the children's pages.

6. **When the clips are fetched, and when one is skipped.** The screen fetches the winners' name clips and the phrase when it hears the final begin — never before a game is over, never in the lobby — and only when its sound is on: a muted or still locked screen fetches nothing. It keeps them decoded in memory for that final, never in `localStorage` or another store. A clip that has not arrived a quarter second before its time (`NAME_LEAD_MS`) is skipped, and one that arrives later is dropped. The cheer follows ADR 025 and 028 like every cue: only for a page that saw the final begin (a reload, the screen opened on the final, a late join say nothing); it stops with its moment (a new game, the room closed, the page hidden or left), and the device's mute silences it. A phone never fetches or plays a name or the phrase: its speaker has no such cue, its props no phrase, its player refuses one, and the route refuses its session.

7. **No contract change.** The state does not say whether a name has a clip: the screen asks for the first place's clips by `player_id` and a 404 is silence. The state stays as it is (`v: 3` since ADR 029), its DTOs, zod schemas and fixtures unchanged. No account or room setting, no migration; nothing is written — opening a clip is not a read of the room (`RecordRoomRead`).

8. **Privacy.** Nothing new is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere: the name is already on the screen, and now it is also heard in the room the screen is in. `/privacy`'s «Böyüklərin oyunları» gains one paragraph (`privacy.rooms.voice`), az and en: «Oyunun sonunda aparıcının böyük ekranı qalibin adını, ad Elifba-nın hazır ad lüğətindədirsə, əvvəlcədən yazılmış səslə deyə bilər. Ad heç bir xidmətə göndərilmir və bunun üçün heç nə saxlanılmır; lüğətdə olmayan ad deyilmir. Ekranın «Səs» düyməsi onu da susdurur.» / "At the end of a game, the host's big screen can say the winner's first name aloud in a prerecorded voice, if the name is in Elifba's built-in name list. The name is sent to no service and nothing is stored for it; a name that is not in the list is not said. The screen's “Sound” button silences it too." The player role's agreement sentence does not change: it already says that the name is shown to the room.

## Consequences

- A room hears its winner named and cheered, in the same voice and tone, from the same clips, as a child at the end of a home game; an adult whose first name the list lacks — many, since the list holds children's names — hears «Əla oynadın!» without it, and a name added to the list is said once ADR 016's process has made its clip.
- The screen makes up to four more requests at a final, each a few kilobytes, the names' with ids only; a 404 for a name without a clip is the normal answer and is not an error.
- The screen's mute is the only way to silence the cheer, and it silences the whole room's sound: there is no setting for the cheer alone.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A new phrase around the name** («Qalib: Nihat!», «Təbriklər, Nihat!»): a new clip; the owner's decision. The board's existing end phrase is no new clip, and the owner asked for it.
- **The name alone** (the first build): the owner heard it in a live game and asked for the board's end, «Nihat! … Əla oynadın!».
- **Speech synthesis or a provider for a name off the list:** it would send an account's name to a third party (ADR 015, 016) or say it in a voice that is not Azerbaijani; a name off the list stays silent.
- **A name at each reveal, for the second and the third, or as a player joins:** the owner's decision; and a name at a reveal would single out a player before the room's final.
- **A flag in the state saying whether a name has a clip** (`has_voice` as on the board): a contract change (DTO, schema, fixtures) for what the clip's own 404 tells the screen at the one moment it asks.
- **The route only for the final's first place:** it would compute the ranking to serve a file and race the next game's start, and protect nothing the same session's state does not already show.
- **A URL with the clip's hash, or a public file:** the hash can be computed from the public list, so the URL would carry the name (ADR 016 decision 8, ADR 024 decision 5).
- **Fetching the clips with the lobby or the screen's props:** a clip would travel before anyone has won, for players who may never win.
- **Reusing the board's route or `Play\Support\PlayerVoice`:** `Rooms` uses only `Language`, `Content` and `DenseRank`, and a room's member is not a game's player.

## What would make us revisit

A room that wants the second and the third named, a wish to hear a name that is not on the list (a larger list for adults, voiced in ADR 016's way), or a host who wants the cheer silent and the rest of the sound on.

## As heard (2026-11-19)

The owner listened on the host's screen in their own Chrome, on a scratch instance of the branch (`odd_one_out`, «Asan», 5 rounds; two players answering by script, the host not playing). The first build said the name alone after the fanfare («Nihat!», and «Nihat! … Aygün!» for a shared first place); the owner asked for the board's end phrase after it, as a home game ends. The second build said «Nihat! … Əla oynadın!» for a single winner, and the owner approved. The numbers stay as written: the cheer at `winnerAt + FANFARE_MS` (2.30 s), `VOICE_GAIN` 0.66, `PHRASE_GAP_MS` 150 ms, `NAME_GAP_MS` 300 ms.

## Amended 2026-11-19 — every phone cheers in a room without a screen

**Why.** The owner, after playing on elifba.io with phones only: players mostly play on their phones, so what only the big screen did must also happen on the phones when there is no screen ("bu tip yalnız böyük ekran qaydalarını ləğv et").

1. **Decision 1** reads: only at the final, only for the first place — on the screen, and on every phone of a room without a screen (the state's `room.screen` false, `carriesRoom()`, ADR 028 as amended the same day). With a screen on, the screen alone cheers, as before: a room hears one voice, not one per phone.
2. **Decision 3** holds at the pace of the final the page shows: a phone's final is quicker (`finalTimeline(…, 'phone')`), so its places, roll and fanfare follow the phone's own final and the cheer comes `FANFARE_MS` after the phone's `winnerAt`. The cheer plays at the phone's level times `VOICE_GAIN`, under the phone's own mute.
3. **Decision 5** reads: the route passes `can:hear` (`RoomPolicy::hear`) — the host or a present player of an open room. A player who left or was removed, a closed room and an account outside the room are refused as before; the route still takes ids only and writes nothing. The phone's page gets `finish` in its props, as the screen does.
4. **Decision 8** reads: the name is heard in the room the screen is in, or from the phones of a game played without one; `/privacy`'s `privacy.rooms.voice` says so, and that the phone's «Səs» silences it too. Nothing new is stored or sent.
5. **The final's confetti** (ADR 025 decision 9) follows the same rule: in a room without a screen it bursts on every phone, as it bursts on the screen for the room; with a screen, on the screen and the winner's own phone only.
