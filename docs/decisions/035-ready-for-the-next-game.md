- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-21, from the owner's words below; everything the owner did not say is this record's recommendation, taken under the owner's standing rule of 2026-11-18 (a session that would ask the owner takes its recommended option and says so) — "Taken without asking" lists it. Decision 6 holds: the mark is no message (decision 11).

## Context

The owner, on 2026-11-21: «bütün oyunlarda otaqda yenidən oynamağa hazırsanmı düyməsi gəlsin ki, misal oyunçu oynamaq istədiyini otaq quran bilsin».

What there is (ADR 026 decision 16, architecture §12 "Games first"):

- **Between games a player's phone only waits.** The start screen says «Qoşuldunuz», the choice's row read-only, «:count oyunçu qoşulub», «Dostunu çağır», «Oyundan çıx», and the bar's waiting line «Aparıcı başladanda oyun başlayacaq.»; after a game the final and «Aparıcı növbəti oyunu seçir.» (`rooms.show.waiting`, `rooms.show.waiting_next`). Nothing a player can say from there.
- **The host sees who is present, not who wants to play.** The start screen lists the present players as chips (`JoinedChips`, with a ✕ for removing); after a game the host's page is the final with «Yenidən oyna» and the other games, and lists nobody. The screen has the same chips (`PlayerChips`) on its poster and none after a game.
- **The precedents.** A per-player fact the host or the player decides is a column of `room_players` written by one action and shown through the state (`voice_admitted_at`, `voice_muted_at`: `SetRoomPlayerVoice`, ADR 031); a switch is `PUT /rooms/{room}/…` `{on}` under the room row's lock with the revision raised and `room.changed` after the commit, answered 204 (`SetRoomTaunts`); a tap posts and the page waits for the state (`useRoomChoice()`, `useTauntsSwitch()`). The room's whole truth reaches devices through the state; the channel carries only `room.changed`; a room page sends no client event.

## Decision

### Who and when

1. **A present player of an open room who is not the host**, while no game is running — before the room's first game, and after a game's end or final: a race over by its timetable or ended by the host, a match over by its outcome. A host who plays too («Mən də oynayıram») has no mark: the host is the one who starts. In a match every present player may mark, seated or watching: the mark says "I want to play the next game", not which seat — the seats stay the choice's (ADR 034 decision 7).
2. **The mark never gates anything.** «Başla» and «Yenidən oyna» are enabled with nobody ready exactly as before; a start takes every present player (a match its two seats) whether they marked or not; nothing starts by itself when everyone is ready; nothing reminds, counts down or sounds. Readiness tells the host; the host decides.

### The fact

3. **`room_players.ready_for`** (an integer, nullable): the position of the game the player said «Hazıram» for — the room's next game at the tap, which is the number of games the room has had. Null: no mark. It holds no moment: nothing needs the time of a person's tap.
4. **One writer, and the leave.** Only `Rooms\Actions\SetRoomPlayerReady` sets or clears it, from `PUT /rooms/{room}/ready` `{on}` (`RoomReadyData::rules()`: `on` required, boolean; `RoomReadyController`), and `LeaveRoom` clears it in the same save as `left_at`. The route is `RoomPolicy::ready` — `play` (a present player of an open room), not the host, no game running at `RoomClock::nowMs()` — so the host, a player who left, a closed room and a running game get 403, and an account the host removed gets 404 (`{room}` does not bind). Under the room row's lock the action asks again — a closed room, a row that is not present, the host's row, a game running — and refuses with `AuthorizationException` (403), as `ChooseRoomGame` and `StartRoomGame` refuse a write a start overtook. Asking for what already counts writes nothing and signals nothing (idempotent). A change writes the row, marks the room active (`last_activity_at`), raises the revision and signals `room.changed` after the commit; the answer is 204 and nothing else — every device, the tapping one too, learns it from the state. `throttle:room-ready`, 30 a minute per account.
5. **Whether it counts is derived** (`Rooms\Support\RoomReady::counts()`): the room is open, the row is present and not the host's, and `ready_for` is the room's next game's position (`RoomReady::next()`: the latest game's position + 1, or 0 before the first). So **a start takes every mark down without writing** — the next game's position moves past them; while a game runs no mark counts; a removed player, an account that is gone and a closed room have none. A player who left and comes back with the code comes back unmarked: the leave cleared it, since the row keeps no moment of a return — `joined_at` is the first join, and it orders the seats and the ranking.

### The state

6. **`players[].ready: boolean`**, last in each player: true exactly when the mark counts (decision 5); false for the host's row, an absent player and every row of a closed room. The state is **`v: 7`** (`RoomStateData::VERSION`, `ROOM_STATE_VERSION`); `RoomPlayerData` gains `ready`, zod `RoomPlayer` gains `ready: z.boolean()`, and every `state.*.json` gains it on each player — true for the viewer of `state.lobby.json` (built from rows by `RoomStateTest`) and for one player of `state.over.json`, false elsewhere. A page that meets `v: 6` reloads itself once (G2). No client event, nothing on the channel but `room.changed`; the route has `{room}` alone, so no name or player id is in a URL.

### The pages

7. **A player's phone** (`PlayerRoom`: the start screen and the final after a game). In the `RoomActionBar`, under the waiting line: «Oynamağa hazırsınız?» before the room's first game (the state's `game` is null), «Yenidən oynamağa hazırsınız?» after one; under it one toggle (`ReadyToggle`, `aria-pressed`, 52px, the bar's width): not pressed, `outline` with `Hand` and «Hazıram»; pressed — the state's `ready` — the `default` button with `Check` and «Hazırsınız». A tap on the pressed one takes the mark back. A tap posts at once and shows the asked value until the state's `ready` moves (`useRoomReady()`, as `useTauntsSwitch()`); a refusal or a failure shows «Göndərilmədi — yenidən toxunun.» and the state's value stands. Never in a round, on the host's phone, on the join page or in a closed room.
8. **The host's phone** (`HostRoom`). On the start screen each ready player's chip (`JoinedChips`) shows `Check` with «Hazır» after the name, in the room's right colour — the word and the icon, never the colour alone. After a game, under the final, «Qoşulanlar (:count)» and the same chips without a ✕ (removing stays where it was: the start screen's chips). By the start, in the bar above «Başla · :count oyunçu» or «Yenidən oyna»: `Check` and «:ready / :total hazırdır», muted — `:total` the present players who are not the host — shown while there is at least one.
9. **The screen** (`rooms/screen`): `PlayerChips` show the same mark, larger; before a game the count stands in the bottom strip before «Dəyiş» and «Başla»; after a game, the right column shows the count under «Yenidən oyna» and the chips under it, above «Başqa oyun:».
10. **No motion and no sound.** A mark appears and goes with the state, without an animation — it is not a game's moment (ADR 025) — and no device plays anything for it: no file is added to `content/rooms/sounds.json`.

### Privacy

11. **What is kept, and what it is not.** `ready_for` is a fact about the player's own wish to play the next game, given with one fixed button, seen by the room's host and present players and on its screen — like whether one is present. It is no message: it names nobody, holds no text and is aimed at nobody, so ADR 024 decision 6 ("No contact between people") holds. It stays on the row until the player taps again or leaves, and goes with the row (ADR 022 decision 7). `privacy.rooms.stored`'s «Otaq:» line names it in both languages (decision 12).

### Words

12. **Every new or changed string**, in `lang/az.json` and `lang/en.json` (+ new, ~ changed). «Oyun» for what a person does and no «otaq» but on `/privacy` (ADR 026 decision 2); none promises a benefit; `RoomsWordingTest` reads this table.

### What does not change

13. Every rule of ADRs 019–034 holds but the lines amended above: what is chosen and how (`ChooseRoomGame`, `rooms.setup_*`), who starts and when (`RoomPolicy::start`, `StartRoomGame`), the seats and their turn, the lock, the removal, the leave (but for the cleared mark), the race's rounds and waits, scoring, taunts, the strip, the final, the cheer, the sounds and the live voice. A class game, a home game, a board, a student and a child get nothing of it.

## Taken without asking

- **A position, not a moment** (decision 3): the plan said `ready_ms`, a mark counting while later than the room's last start and the player's present join. Two things stand against a moment: a game's `started_ms` is the end of its countdown, and a game may end before it — the host's «Oyunu bitir» in the countdown, a seat leaving a match whose clock has not started — so a mark made after such an end would stay hidden until that moment passed; and a row keeps no moment of a return (`joined_at` is the first join and orders the seats and the ranking). A position is exact and keeps no time of anyone's tap.
- **The leave clears it** (decisions 4–5): the one write beside `SetRoomPlayerReady`'s, so a return starts unmarked; a start, a removal and a close write nothing for it.
- **Every present player but the host**, seated or watching in a match (decision 1).
- **After a game the host sees the chips under the final** (decision 8), without a ✕, and the screen in its right column (decision 9); the count stands by the start on both.
- **A change marks the room active**, as the host's switch does (decision 4), and **30 a minute** per account bounds the toggling.
- **No motion, no sound, no gate, no auto-start** (decisions 2 and 10).

## Alternatives rejected

- **A client event or a channel message** («hazıram» sent phone to phone or on `room.{id}`): CLAUDE.md "Rooms" — the channel carries only `room.changed`, and a room page sends no client event. The mark is a fact in a row like every other.
- **A moment** (`ready_ms`): "Taken without asking".
- **Clearing every mark in `StartRoomGame`**: a write per player at every start for what the next game's position already says.
- **Starting by itself when everyone is ready, or holding «Başla» until someone is**: the owner asked that the host _know_; the host still decides when, and may start with whoever is there.
- **A mark for the host**, or the host marking for someone: the host starts; a mark is the player's own.

## Consequences

- A player has something to say between games, in one tap, and can take it back; the host and the screen see who wants to play before starting.
- The state is `v: 7`: a page loaded before the deploy reloads once when it meets it.
- `privacy.rooms.stored` changes: `/privacy` says it in both languages.

## What would make us revisit

- The host asks to start by itself when everyone is ready, or to remind those who are not.
- People read «Hazır» on the chips as a seat or a turn in a match.
