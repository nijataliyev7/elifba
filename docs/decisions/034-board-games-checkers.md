# 034 — Board games in a room: checkers, two players and the room watching

- **Status:** Proposed, 2026-11-20; the clock, the standing, taunts, the screen and sound added the same day (decisions 9–13), the owner's choices
- **Sections:** architecture.md §1 "Tables" (`rooms.board`, `room_matches`, `room_moves`), §12 "Board games"
- **Amends:** [023](023-room-games-and-content.md) decision 2 (a room game is `mechanic × difficulty`): a room may instead be opened for a **board game**, which is not a mechanic. [021](021-room-race-protocol.md) is unchanged for round games; a match follows its own, simpler protocol below. [026](026-games-first.md): «Oyunlar» lists the board games after the round games.
- **Why it is hard to reverse:** the tables, the move a phone posts and the rule that only the server judges a move.

## Context

The owner asked for checkers («şaşki») among the adults' games (2026-11-20) and chose Russian draughts on 8 × 8, two players with everyone else in the room watching. Every room game so far is a race: everyone answers the same round at once, rounds are timed and built when the game starts (ADR 021–023). Checkers has no rounds, no timer and no difficulty; two people take turns on one board until one side cannot move. Fitting it into `RoomMechanic` would have given every round-game reader — the timetable, the judge, the ranking, the taunts, the levels file — a case that means nothing to it.

## Decision

1. **A board game is not a mechanic.** `Rooms\Enums\RoomBoardGame` lists them (`checkers`). A room has a nullable `rooms.board`; null is the round games as before.
2. **Opening.** «Oyunlar» shows «Şaşki» («Taxta oyunları») after the six round games; choosing it posts `board: checkers` to `rooms.store`. `OpenRoom` opens a room with `board` set, or turns the host's open room to it (and back to the round games when a round game is chosen) unless a game or a match is running. The room keeps its round-game choice (`setup_*`), which a board room never starts: `RoomPolicy::start` refuses a board room.
3. **A match.** The host picks two present players — white and black — and starts (`POST /rooms/{room}/match`, `StartRoomMatch`, `RoomPolicy::startMatch`). One match runs at a time; a room plays several, by `position`. Everyone else in the room, and the host when not playing, watches the same board.
4. **The rules are code, PHP only** (`Rooms\Support\Checkers`): men step forward and capture both ways; kings fly; capturing is compulsory and goes on while it can, any capturing path may be chosen; a king landing after a capture must take a square from which it can go on capturing, when there is one; captured pieces stay on the board until the move ends (never jumped twice, they block); a man reaching the far row is crowned at once and a capture under way goes on as a king's. White moves first.
5. **A move.** Only the player to move posts it, `POST /rooms/{room}/match/moves` `{event_id, ply, path}`, and only `PlayRoomMove` writes it, under the room row's lock: idempotent by `event_id` (`duplicate`); `late` when no match runs, it is not the player's turn or `ply` is not the match's; 422 when the path is no legal move; a watcher gets 403. The path is the start square and every landing. An accepted move updates the position (`board`, `turn`, `ply`, `quiet_plies`, `last_path`), raises the room's revision and signals `room.changed` — nothing else travels on the channel.
6. **The end.** The side to move has no legal move: the other side wins (`no_moves`). The side to move runs out of time: the other side wins (`time`, decision 9). Thirty quiet plies in a row — kings' moves without a capture — draw (`quiet`). A player resigns (`POST …/match/resign`) or leaves or is removed: the other side wins (`resign`, `left`). The host ends a match (`DELETE …/match`) or the room closes: no result (`host`). A match counts in no ranking.
7. **What a device reads.** `GET /rooms/{room}/match` (`watch`, `no-store`; `RoomMatchStateData`): the revision, the viewer's side, and the latest match with its position, turn, last move, result, and while it runs the side to move's legal moves and whether they are captures. A page offers only those moves — a tap on a piece, then each landing, until the taps name a whole path — and never computes one. Everything in it is what every watcher sees on the board anyway, so it is served to every watcher alike. Names come from the room state; a player is known by `room_players.id`.
8. **The page.** `rooms/show` draws `CheckersRoom` in the game frame for a board room: outside a match the last result, the invitation, who joined and — for the host — the two sides and «Başla»; during a match the board (turned round for black), the two players with their pieces left and whose turn it is, «Təslim ol» for a player and «Oyunu bitir» for the host. The page follows the room state (`useLiveRoom()`) and reads the match again whenever the revision rises.

9. **The clock** (the owner: five minutes a player). Each side has `elifba.rooms.checkers.clock_ms` (300 000) that runs only on its turn: `white_left_ms`, `black_left_ms` and `turn_since_ms`, which `StartRoomMatch` sets and `PlayRoomMove` moves on — the mover keeps what it had left. A side whose time runs out has lost, **derived, never written by a job** (`RoomMatch::settled()`, applied by `Room::latestMatch()`): every reader sees it at once, a move after it is `late`, and the next write — the next start — saves it. The match read gives each side's time left at `server_ms`; the page counts the side to move's down and reads the match again when it reaches zero, since no revision tells of a flag.
10. **The standing** (the owner: into the room's standing). A won match gives the winner `RoomPointsRule::MATCH_WIN` (3), a draw each side `MATCH_DRAW` (1), a match the host ended nothing; `RoomRanking::ofRoom()` adds them to the round games' points, so the room's standing, «Keçmiş oyunlar» and a closed room's results show them. A board room's state carries the room's standing from its first finished match.
11. **Taunts** (the owner: with a cooldown). A player of the running match throws the other a taunt — a key of `content/rooms/taunts.json`, as in a round game — through `POST …/match/taunts` `{event_id, taunt}` and `ThrowMatchTaunt` only; the target is always the opponent. It is free (a match has no points to pay with), limited by `elifba.rooms.taunts.cooldown_ms`, behind the host's switch (`rooms.taunts`), and `late` for a watcher. Rows are `room_match_taunts`; the target is emptied after `forget_target_hours` (`PruneOldRooms`). The match read gives the viewer whether they may throw and their cooldown, the taunts aimed at them within `show_ms`, and the screen (`?for=screen`, `RoomPolicy::screen`) every one with its target. The phone draws the received ones with `TauntReaction`, the screen with `ScreenTauntReaction`; the button is on the opponent's line.
12. **Sound.** Only the room's existing files (`content/rooms/sounds.json`): the screen — and a phone in a room without a screen — plays «go» when a match starts, «question» when a move passes the turn and «fanfare» when a match ends, each only for a moment the page saw happen; a phone's own taps play «tap», «undo» and, for a move accepted, «sent». Each device keeps its mute (`useMatchSound()`).
13. **The big screen.** `rooms/screen` draws `ScreenCheckers` for a board room: read-only — the host starts from the phone — the board from white's side at full height with both players' pieces and clocks, the taunts and the standing; outside a match the QR, the code, the last result and the standing.

## Consequences

- A board game is a builder of nothing, a rules class, a page and a few rows; another board game is another `RoomBoardGame` case with its own rules class.
- A match ends at the latest when the side to move's time is out; a room still closes after `idle_hours` without a move.
- No motion of ADR 025, rivalry strip or winner's cheer in a match; the live voice works as in any room, being the room's.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Checkers as a `RoomMechanic`:** every round-game reader would grow a case with no meaning for it.
- **Judging moves on the phone:** against "the server alone judges"; the legal moves are sent instead, which costs nothing since the board is public.
- **Pairing everyone into simultaneous matches:** the owner chose two players and spectators.
